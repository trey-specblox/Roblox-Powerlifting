## The exterior (two photos in chat, not saved as files)

A long, low exhibition hall seen from its front corner:
- **A row of repeated gabled roof bays** (about 6–7 peaks in a line), each peak a pitched roof with **exposed steel truss frames** (white or grey lattice girders) standing out in front of the roof edge, like a row of tents. Roofs are **teal-green metal**, with white trim.
- **The front wall is mostly glass** (tall curtain-wall windows) behind the trusses, glowing warm from inside at night.
- **A brick / warm-brown base**, wood-look panels and white wall sections at the ends.
- **Steel columns** along the front holding the trusses, with **lamp heads** on them; a covered walkway at the far end.
- Night photo: deep blue sky, warm interior light through the glass, lamps lit.
- Fits the project rules: **pitched roofs, no flat roof, broken-up shape** (memory `no-flat-roofs`).

## The interior (photos 01–04, 08: one civic-center venue)

- A **big flat-floored hall**, polished grey concrete floor.
- **Exposed blue steel roof trusses** across the ceiling, with **rows of high-bay lights**; white or cream walls.
- **Spectators:** fixed **blue stadium seats rising in tiers** along the long sides (about 15–20 rows), with **blue railings**, aisles and stairs, and an **upper concourse / balcony** at the back of the seating. More seats on lower floor-level rows. White **curtain drapes** close off one end of the hall.
- **The floor in front of the seats is the lifters' space:** a **rail or barricade line** separates the stands from the competition floor (photo 01 from above: spectators lean on the rail right above a platform; 02–03: spectators sit close behind it).
- On the floor: platforms with a power rack or bench, **judges' tables** (black skirted tables, **red padded chairs**), loaders' plate trees, a **logo mat** where lifters wait, and the next lifters standing ready close to the platform.

## The State Championship photos (05–07): space

- A big **overhead banner** ("STATE CHAMPIONSHIP" style, navy with the state logo) above the officials' area.
- A **branded backdrop wall** (logo repeat) for the awards / photos, with trophies on a table beside it.
- Each platform has **big black TV screens on draped stands** (black curtains round the stand) and a **"Platform N" sign** on a skirted table.
- **Lots of floor space:** the lifters' section is wide open around each platform (coaches, the next lifters, loaders), and **the spectators are kept back** behind rails in the stands. The floor feels busy but never crowded onto the platform.

## Lighting (photos 10, 11)

- **The room is dark.** The seats fade to black.
- **The platform is lit hard from above** (a spotlight): the lifter, bar and bench stand out bright against the dark; strong shadows on the floor.
- **Coloured backlight on drapes:** purple / violet light on the black curtains by the walk-on, a cool blue on the back wall, chalk dust glowing in the light.
- Black drapes and a dark backdrop frame the platform.

## The meet while lifting (YouTube: 2026 USA Powerlifting Collegiate Nationals, Day 1 Session 1, Platforms 1 & 2)

Coverage: the whole 3 h 56 min broadcast, a frame every 3 minutes (every minute for the first 16; the first 12:30 and two breaks are a title card), about 85 frames, plus one deadlift sequence frame by frame (every 6 s, 3:40:30–3:41:36). Broadcast at 480p; fine detail like small text was not readable.

- **Two platforms** side by side (the broadcast shows both split-screen, later one at a time in a wide shot).
- **Each platform is a low raised stage** (a step high) with a **branded front edge**, a dark rubber top, the rack or bench in the middle.
- **Behind it, a tall printed backdrop wall** in the event colours (purple with gold and orange flashes and the event logo repeated).
- **Above the backdrop, a huge LED video wall** showing the lifter live, with their name, school and attempt beside the picture.
- **Dark drapes** (deep red / black) hang at both sides of the stage, closing off the wings where lifters wait and walk on from.
- **Loaders / spotters:** three on squat and bench (one each side, one behind), all in **matching uniforms** (black tee, blue shorts); two strip and load plates between attempts from **plate trees** at the sides of the stage; for deadlift they step right back.
- **The head referee** sits in front of the stage in a **red chair**, side referees at the corners; the head ref's arm signals ("down", "rack").
- **A lift, frame by frame:** the previous lifter leaves; loaders change the plates within a few seconds; three white lights; the next lifter walks on **from the side wing**, alone in the middle, chalked; sets up; lifts; the head ref signals; the lifter walks off the same side; the loaders move in again. The stage is clear around the lifter during the lift.
- The broadcast's own score overlays (names, attempts, standings) sit on the picture; in the game the video wall and the meet screens do that job (memory `no-screen-clutter`).
