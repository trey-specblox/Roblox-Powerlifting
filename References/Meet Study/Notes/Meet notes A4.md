## 10. Surprises / what a game would get wrong
1. Men's bench celebrations are SHORT and happen while WALKING OFF, mostly before the lights (M1-M6). Typical size: one
   fist up, a roar at the camera, a double fist pump, one flex. Only the national record (M8) got arms-to-the-sky + hugs.
   A game that plays a big 5-second celebration on every good opener will look wrong. Scale with the situation: opener =
   nothing or a nod; PB 3rd attempt = roar/flex/arms wide; record = sky-point + coach hugs.
2. The biggest PB of the session (275, 134:04) got NO reaction at all. Calm strolls are normal for some lifters.
3. Lifters sit up from the bench with a LEG-SWING rock-up (both legs kicked up, then forward), especially big men
   (124:51, 127:29, 129:12, 136:58, 141:26, 143:27). Then sit 1 s with hands on knees, then stand.
4. First thing after the lift: they pull the wrist-wrap velcro open while walking off (121:45, 131:15, 134:06, 140:29).
5. Misses are quiet: no anger, no head in hands. Sit, look down 1 s, walk off, maybe a smile or an open-palm shrug
   (139:42), coach's hand on the shoulder. Bench misses are slow, long grinds (5-8 s) where the bar can sink back down,
   ended by THREE spotters lifting together.
6. Before the attempt, lifters greet the crew (fist bump with the side spotter, thumbs-up, high five with a loader -
   121:18, 130:48-130:50, 140:02-140:04) and some explain the hand-off to the center spotter (133:28-133:36).
7. Pre-lift rituals are calm and personal, not hype: sitting on the bench end hands on knees 5 s; bent-over stare; arm
   swings; the arms-spread fall-back onto the bench (132:37, 142:08); headphones until the last second; ammonia from the
   coach's zip bag. Nobody slaps or shouts on the way to the bench in this bench session.
8. The decision is presented by the venue, not just by lights: the LED wall flashes "GOOD LIFT", the exit wall reads
   GOOD LIFT / NO LIFT, and lifters often learn a no-lift backstage looking at a monitor (130:12-130:25).
9. Deadlift setups are long (10-20 s in the bottom with hip wedging) and the lifter holds the lockout ~1 s before a
   controlled lower; no drops. The women's walk-on is announced by a giant portrait + name on the LED wall.
10. The coach meeting at the exit is part of every attempt: fist bump, palm clasp, hug with back slap, or the coach
   talking hard into the lifter's face/ear; for the record a second coach holds his head and talks into his ear.
