## 10. What surprised me / what a game would most likely get wrong
- The stage is DARK: a black theatre with one top light on the platform; lifters are lit like a spotlight act, not a
  bright gym (eu_9nPA3zRs 19:13-19:59). Chat complained ("why is it so dark").
- Lifters stand still under the bar ~9-11 s after unracking before the descent (19:37-19:48, 21:55-22:04). A game
  that squats immediately after the walkout looks rushed.
- Openers get almost no reaction: the lifter just walks off (19:55-19:57).
- A depth miss gets a calm walk-off with one look back at the lights (22:13-22:14.5), not drama.
- Four spotter/loaders on stage at all times, plus a head ref out front; five for the heaviest bars, and they
  crouch WITH the lifter on every rep (25:00-25:03). A game with static spotters will look wrong.
- Each lifter repeats her OWN small ritual every attempt (raised arm on entry for one lifter at 21:36 and 30:27;
  forward belt tug for another at 23:41 and 31:33; hair gather at 22:41 and 32:33). Give each lifter one signature
  move and repeat it, rather than random rituals.
- Celebrations are done walking away from the bar toward the wing, then finished with the coach (hug / fist bump)
  just behind the wall (32:13.5, 35:07, 39:22.5, 40:40.5). The platform is left almost immediately.
- Lifters look back over their shoulder at the lights while walking off (22:13, 24:15, 26:10) rather than
  standing and staring at the light box.
- The record attempt is staged differently: banner, coach hype on deck, slower grind, LED "NEW AMERICAN RECORD",
  run-and-jump hug (39:37-40:43).
- Time with the bar on the back before the descent is long (8-11 s) and the walkout is short (2-3 steps).
