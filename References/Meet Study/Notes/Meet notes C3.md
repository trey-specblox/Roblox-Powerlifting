## 4. Before the platform, entrances and walk-ups, rituals
- Backstage coaching (in every broadcast that shows backstage): coach at the shoulder talking into the ear while the

  (9FQ 3:02-3:13; jfM 2:18-2:22), cupping the lifter's face with both hands (jfM 3:04-3:09), a second coach adjusting

- Warm-up: bouncing on the toes, shaking out the arms, rubbing the hands, headphones off at the last moment (9FQ

## 5. Going up to the bar, holding the bar, setup

  long set of 3-6 s with the HIPS HIGH and the arms straight (oGfa 0:33.5-0:36, 2:34.5-2:39, 5:28-5:34), then the hips
  sink and the chest/head come up in the last ~0.5 s before the bar breaks (oGfa 0:36.25-0:36.75, 1:17-1:18.25).
  Re-sets are common: grip, stand back up, re-grip (9FQ 0:19-0:26; oGfa 4:16-4:21; gfp 10:08-10:09). Sumo lifters take a
  very wide stance with the hands inside the knees (9FQ 4:01-4:06; jfM 3:20-3:24). The 1:00 attempt clock runs during

## 6. The lift itself
- Easy deadlift: floor to lockout 0.5-1.0 s (oGfa 0:37-0:38, 1:37-1:37.5, 4:50.25-4:50.75).
- Grinder deadlift: 2.5-6 s. oGfa 7:38-7:40.5 (bar crawls up the shins, lips pulled back, cheeks puffed, holding the
  breath; stalls at the upper thigh with the knees still bent, never locks). jfM 0:40.5-0:47 (hips stuck behind the
  bar ~3 s at the top, face to the ceiling, mouth open). gfp 10:20-10:30 (about 4 s from the floor to the knees, 2 s
  more to lockout, screams at the top). 9FQ 5:47.5-5:54 (teeth bared). 9FQ 1:07.5-1:09 (very slow off the floor).
- Lockout HOLD waiting for "down": 1-4 s (oGfa 0:38-0:39 ~1.5 s, 8:42-8:45.5 ~4 s; 9FQ 4:56-4:59.5 ~3.5 s). The head
  very often TILTS BACK during the hold, face to the ceiling (oGfa 4:02.75-4:04.5, 8:42-8:45.5, 9:49.25-9:51; 9FQ
  1:37-1:39, 2:15-2:17.5, 4:09-4:12.5). One lifter yells at the ceiling during the hold (9FQ 6:46-6:48).
- Putting it down: mostly a fast guided drop; the lifter stays bent over it for a moment (oGfa 0:39.5-0:40.5, 1:22,
  4:05 dreadlocks flying).
- Squat: descent ~1.5-2 s, slow grind up 2-4 s on records (gfp 1:39.5-1:47, 2:19-2:28; jfM 5:16-5:27). Very deep.
- Bench: press 3-5 s with a pause on the chest (gfp 4:46-4:51, 5:56-6:00).

## 7. Completing, the lights, misses and reactions to a miss
- Lights: the IPF graphic box (bottom right) shows a single dot when the bar is down, then 3 circles ~0.5 s later,
  about 2-3 s after lockout (oGfa 0:40.5-0:41, 7:43). Sheffield shows ticks/crosses in the lower-third box ~1.5-3 s
  after the bar is down (9FQ 0:38.5, 1:15, 1:43.5, 6:51.5). US meets show 3 dots at the top (gfp 1:06, 2:29).
  Lifters rarely LOOK at the lights: big celebrations start before the lights in at least 8 cases (9FQ 0:36.5,
  1:13.5, 2:19.5, 6:46; gfp 1:04, 9:15; jfM 4:28; and several walk-offs) and calm lifters simply walk off.
- Misses where the bar never moves: the lifter stands up, leaves the bar, stands a moment and walks off quietly (oGfa
  4:25.5-4:31, 6:57.5-7:04, 11:49.25-11:56); the coach meets him with an arm round the shoulders or a hug.

  head down, walks off (oGfa 7:40.75-7:44).
- Grip miss: the bar slips out of the hands near lockout; frustrated arm swing, a jump, falls to the knees with the
  head on the platform, then walks away (jfM 1:36-1:41, front replay 1:55-1:58).
- Knee-stall miss: stuck at the knees ~1.5 s, lowers it, head tilted, lips pressed (oGfa 10:28-10:36).
- The crowd cheers a missed world-record attempt anyway (oGfa 11:50-11:52).

- IPF Worlds: lifters walk off to the side (left or right, behind an ESN banner); a loader walking in often slaps or
  touches the lifter's hand as they pass (oGfa 0:42, 4:59). Backstage a coach meets them: handshake into a half hug
  (0:44), hand slap (1:28), high-five into a hug (3:26), bear hug rocking (9:56-9:58), arm round the shoulders after a
  miss (4:29.5).
- Sheffield: a handheld camera follows the walk-off past the WORLD RECORD tunnel wall; hugs with the coach are long
  and rocking (9FQ 5:11-5:14, 6:58-7:01.5); belts are taken off and swung (9FQ 0:42.5-0:43.5).
- Women are lifted off their feet by coaches several times (9FQ 2:57-2:58.5; gfp 3:52, 11:27-11:30).
- Loaders strip and reload within seconds of the lifter leaving: two at the collars, one carrying (oGfa 1:42-1:44,

## 9. The meet
- IPF Worlds (oGfa; gfp 3:18, 5:32, 7:34; most of jfM): white backdrop with a grey world map and big sponsor logos,
  raised dark platform on a bright blue carpet, plate trees/benches at the platform's back corners, side referee in a
  chair with a lights button box at the platform corner (gfp 6:06-6:09), a referee table in front (jfM 3:10-3:16),
  three loaders in black, a packed stand of red seats with flags waved. Broadcast: lifter cards, standings tables, an
  attempt board with a WORLD RECORD stamp (oGfa 6:13-6:16), attempt clock, flag/logo wipes between lifters, slow side
  replays after key attempts.
- Sheffield (9FQ; gfp 0:02, 7:06, 11:31): dark concert arena, haze and coloured light, a huge LED back wall that plays
  NEW IPF / WORLD RECORD animations after a record (and GOOD LIFT in green at a US meet, gfp 9:17); spotters stand at
  both ends of the deadlift bar during the pull; steep packed seating; handheld cameras on the stage edge.
- US national meets (gfp 0:22, 4:32; jfM 1:59, 4:50): black stage, red curtains, LED wall with the lifter's name in
  giant letters and a "NEW AMERICAN RECORD" animation (gfp 1:07); loaders in matching white tees; an announcer in a
  hat with a microphone on the floor beside the stage (jfM 5:49-5:51); hotel-ballroom crowds that jump and point;

- French meet: SPARK FOUNTAINS shoot up either side of the platform on a good record lift (gfp 3:11-3:16, 4:24-4:31).
- Commentators: two voices, sometimes on camera at a desk, standing up and celebrating (gfp 1:59-2:04, 2:43-2:45).

## 10. What surprised me / what a game would most likely get wrong
1. The deadlift set is LONG and hips-high: 3-6 s bent over the bar with high hips, then the hips drop only in the last
   half second (oGfa 0:33.5-0:36.75, 5:28-5:34.5). A game will likely crouch and pull at once.
2. The lockout HOLD is long (1-4 s) and the head goes back, face to the ceiling, very often (oGfa 8:42-8:45.5; 9FQ
   1:37-1:39). Hold until "down" and tilt the head up.
3. Celebrations start BEFORE the lights on most big lifts. Waiting for the lights before any reaction looks wrong.
4. The biggest men's celebrations use the FLOOR: one knee (gfp 1:05), face-down dive (gfp 1:52; jfM 4:31, 5:34), back
   roll with the legs in the air (jfM 6:22), forehead to the platform (jfM 3:32.5), head on the plates (jfM 0:51.5).
   A standing fist pump is the low end.
5. Both hands on top of the head ("I can't believe it") is common for men AND women (9FQ 0:38, 6:51.5-6:53.5).
6. Misses are mostly QUIET: stand up, look at the bar, walk off, coach's arm round the shoulders (oGfa 4:25-4:31,
   6:57-7:04). Only near-misses (grinder, grip) get the scream or jump (oGfa 7:41; jfM 1:38-1:41).
7. Coaches and loaders are part of every moment: hand slaps on the way off, rocking hugs, lifting the lifter, loaders
   clapping over a lifter lying on the floor (jfM 5:36-5:41). An empty platform edge looks wrong.
8. A grinder shows: the bar crawls, lips pulled back, cheeks puffed, shoulders behind the bar while the knees are still
   bent (oGfa 7:38-7:40.5). Easy rep 0.5-1 s, grinder 2.5-6 s.
9. Venues differ a lot (white-and-blue IPF hall, dark LED arena with haze, hotel ballroom, spark fountains), and a
   record lift makes the venue react (LED animation, sparks).
10. Crowd work before the lift (oGfa 8:12-8:24) and pre-lift rituals are per-lifter signatures repeated on every
    attempt (the shoulder-slap lifter: 4 times across two videos).
