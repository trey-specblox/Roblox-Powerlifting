## 5. Going up to the bar, holding the bar, setup
SQUAT

  wide on these light men: about 1.7-2x shoulder width, hands near the rack uprights (2:16, 1:21), elbows bent and down,
  thumbs around.

  behind the bar, elbows driven back and UP, bar on the traps/upper back (1:27, 2:26-2:27).

## 6. The lift itself
- Squat tempo: descent ~1.5 s, hole 0.5-1 s, ascent ~1.5-2 s on easy/opener reps (up-LEMKGRA8 0:54-1:00; YLMRf70ALKo
  1:03.5-1:08.5). Depth well below parallel on every good lift. Cheeks puffed / held breath the whole rep (YLMRf70ALKo
  1:03).
- Squat GRINDER (YLMRf70ALKo 3:03-3:11): descent 1.5 s; starts up slowly 3:05.5-3:06; hips rise faster than the chest so
  the torso tips to ~45 deg, face screwed up, SCREAMING (caption); sticking point just above parallel ~1 s (3:07.5-3:08.5);
  chest comes back up (3:09); lockout 3:10.5-3:11, face red. Ascent ~5 s. Spotters hover, never touch.
- Squat "positioning" rep (YMrXwUrTpkw 1:05.5-1:07): knees pitch forward mid-ascent, slower, then snaps up.
- Racking: walks the bar forward into the hooks and FOLDS OVER THE BAR as it racks, head down (up-LEMKGRA8 1:00-1:02);
  some crouch down for a moment right after racking (YLMRf70ALKo 2:13.5, 4:03).
- Bench: lower ~1.5 s, pause on the chest ~1 s, press ~1.5 s, hold for "rack" (up-LEMKGRA8 7:04-7:09). Bench grind
  (YLMRf70ALKo 7:22-7:28): the arms extend a little each second for ~5 s; the bar dipped = no lift.
- Deadlift: conventional opener floor-to-lockout ~2 s (up-LEMKGRA8 12:09-12:11); sumo pulls by light men are fast,
  ~1 s (YLMRf70ALKo 9:07; 14:54-14:55 for B). Lockout = standing tall, shoulders back, knees straight; held 1-3 s for
  "down" (YLMRf70ALKo 12:59-13:01.5 held 3 s on the gold pull). Lowered with the hands on the bar the whole way, ~1-1.5 s,
  then the lifter stays bent over the bar a moment (up-LEMKGRA8 12:12.5-12:14). Never dropped.
- Deadlift grinder (up-LEMKGRA8 15:35-15:42): bar stuck just above the knees ~7 s, knees bent, torso ~45 deg, shoulders
  slightly behind the bar, straining; then lowered.
- Commands are not audible (music/edit), but the pauses match: still standing before "squat"; locked arms before
  "start"; lockout held for "rack"/"down".

## 10. What surprised me / what a game would most likely get wrong
1. Light men grip the squat bar extremely wide (hands near the uprights, ~2x shoulder width) and look DOWN at the
   knurling for several seconds before ducking under (up-LEMKGRA8 2:16-2:21). A game will probably use a narrow, instant grip.
2. They stand under the racked bar 5-10 s before unracking (up-LEMKGRA8 0:34-0:43, 4:08-4:12). Instant unracks look fake.
3. Almost every deadlifter grips, STANDS BACK UP, lets go, breathes, then grips again (up-LEMKGRA8 12:03; YMrXwUrTpkw
   4:28). A one-motion grip-and-pull misses this.
4. Celebrations often start BEFORE the lights (the lifter knows); the biggest ones come on the last lift (YLMRf70ALKo
   13:03; YMrXwUrTpkw 5:49.5). Openers get nothing. Energy should scale with the situation, not be random.
5. The big men's celebrations are body-wide: hands on the head, a crouch, then a V, then hands over the mouth
   (YLMRf70ALKo 13:03-13:11) or arms flung wide with a full scream (YMrXwUrTpkw 3:42, 5:50). Small fist pumps alone
   would undersell it.
6. A spotter crew actively follows every squat with cupped hands under the sleeves, and on a miss the rear spotter
   bear-hugs the lifter from behind (up-LEMKGRA8 4:29.5-4:30.5). Static spotters would look wrong.
7. Misses are not always angry: smiles and laughs are common (up-LEMKGRA8 9:56.5, 16:38.5), and a proud lifter may
   shake a referee's hand and put a hand on his heart (15:54-16:01).
8. The bench setup is a long, personal routine (sit, rub shins, legs-up swing, arch wriggle, open-hand placement) that
   takes 10-20 s (up-LEMKGRA8 6:38-6:47; YLMRf70ALKo 5:53-5:57). Lying straight down and pressing looks robotic.
9. A grinder squat has the hips shooting up first and the torso tipping to ~45 deg with a scream, then the chest coming
   back up (YLMRf70ALKo 3:06.5-3:09); a game "slow rep" that just plays the same motion slower will look wrong.
10. Presentation details that sell "real meet": the green room wash on white lights (Sheffield), the lifter's face on a
   giant LED wall as he walks on, the 1:00 attempt clock and "WORLD RECORD" banner on the scoreboard, a jury overrule,
   overhead top-down camera on bench/deadlift, and the crowd standing on big lifts.
