## 4. Before the platform, entrances, walk-ups, rituals
- Backstage chain: wait by the AVANCUS wall with the coach -> chalk at a pedestal bowl -> walk through a lit tunnel
  -> onto the stage -> the LED wall behind the platform shows the lifter's face + name -> to the bar. A steadicam
  operator often walks right beside the lifter (44:17, 56:40).
- Coach rituals seen: trap kneading from behind for 10+ s (46:12-46:23); gripping the lifter's upper arms and shaking /
  talking face to face (44:04-44:06, 63:08); chest-high hand clasp then lifter roars (49:54-49:55); coach holding an
  AMMONIA capsule under the nose, lifter's head jerks back, grimace, coach yells (65:05-65:08, 74:04-74:07); lifter
  cupping his own hand over nose/mouth for salts (63:06); coach hand flat on the lifter's chest for 8 s talking
  (77:44-77:51); coach chopping-hand pep talk (68:59-69:03).
- Gear rituals: belt closed backstage with both hands on the lever (50:53-51:03, 57:01-57:03); belt closed ON the
  platform in front of the rack (64:15.5-64:17.5: grab, lean, push lever, check); wrist wraps pulled tight (46:15,
  67:01-67:06); chalk hands (53:22-53:30) and forearms (77:28-77:35). Big over-ear HEADPHONES on until the last moment,
  lifted off with both hands (51:47-51:51, 54:53-54:59, 72:54, 74:00).
- On-stage entrances are calm: walk on with arms loose, eyes on the bar, hands on hips or on the belt (55:10-55:14
  both hands on the belt staring at the bar). Only a few hype moves: a woman throwing both arms up then spreading them
  palms up to the crowd (43:23.5-43:24.0); a man swinging his right arm across his body and flinging it out plus one
  clap (52:49-52:50.8, again 72:05); rubbing hands together (54:21); bending with hands on thighs to rest (44:26).
- No belt slaps, no chest slaps before the lift, no shouting at the bar in my range (chest slap only after a lift, 75:54).

## 5. Going up to the bar, holding the bar, setup (squat)
- Grip: they walk up and put the RIGHT hand on the bar first in most clear shots (41:14, 51:16, 52:04, 53:38, 74:20),
  then the left, arms straight out to the sides like a "hands up" pose. Grips are WIDE: hands well outside the
  shoulders, on women and the 84 kg woman nearly at the collars (45:29); heavy men also wide (57:22).
- Many stand IN FRONT of the bar holding it with straight arms for 3-5 s staring ahead or at their hands before ducking
  under (50:11-50:16, 59:31-59:34, 69:15-69:19).
- Duck under: knees bend, head ducks, then they stand UNDER the bar bent ~45 deg forward with the HEAD BOWED staring at
  the floor for a long time: 8-15 s is normal (45:36-45:44, 59:35-59:43, 60:36-60:47, 69:20-69:35 ~15 s, the 320 kg
  woman ~25 s 47:47-48:11). Then head comes up, cheeks puff with a big breath, elbows pull back and they stand it up.
- Walkout: 3 steps (left back, right back and out, adjust) seen in feet close-up 52:08.5-52:10.0; then 4-10 s standing
  set before the descent (46:52-46:56 ~4 s; 56:23-56:25), breathing, cheeks puffed.
- Bar on the upper back, elbows back; stance a little wider than shoulders, toes out.
- Five spotter/loaders around the bar: two at each end and one directly behind who crouches with the lifter.

## 6. The lift itself (squat)
- Typical easy rep: ~1.5 s down, ~1.5-2 s up (51:31-51:35; 52:22-52:25 ~1 s each way; 430 kg at 57:38-57:41: 1.7 s
  down, ~1 s up, explosive). Some lifters always descend very slowly, ~3-4 s (49:24-49:28, 59:51-59:55).
- Grinder: ~1.5 s down then 3-4 s up with a visible STALL just above parallel, often 1-2 s nearly motionless (40:30-
  40:32, 42:49-42:52, 44:57-45:00, 61:53-61:55 bar tilting right-side low, 70:42-70:46 "you see the pause", 75:43-75:46,
  485 kg 78:28-78:31). Faces: mouth open, teeth showing, face red; one lifter's left knee caved in and pushed back out
  (62:50.5-62:51.0).
- Side angle (64:35-64:40): torso leans ~35-40 deg at the bottom, hip crease below the knee; rear spotter crouching
  right behind.
- After lockout they stand still for ~1-2 s (waiting for "rack"), then walk the bar forward into the hooks, and almost
  ALWAYS bend forward over the racked bar with the head dropped for a moment (40:34, 63:43.5, 66:44, 74:49).
- Cheeks puffed (held breath) through the whole rep in close-ups (50:36-50:37, 67:41-67:43).

## 7. Completing, the lights, misses and reactions
- Lights: shown in a small box lower-left of the broadcast graphic and, more visibly, by the whole LED wall: a WHITE
  flash then giant "GOOD LIFT" letters (46:00-46:01, 52:29, 61:07-61:08, 73:39.5-73:40, 78:37.8), or a RED flash /
  "NO LIFT" (47:02.5, 69:53, 71:38.5). The stage lighting itself turns red on a no-lift (69:49.5-69:50.0) and purple
  for a record attempt (77:58-78:01).
- Lifters rarely stop to look at the lights; most are already walking off when they come on. Exception: the 83 kg man
  turning his head to the side judge after a depth call (60:03-60:05, 69:50-69:52) and the 398 man looking at the side
  judge (77:00-77:03).
- Strength misses (5): the lifter stalls 1.5-2 s just above the bottom, torso tips slightly forward, then the side
  spotters (hands already cupped under the sleeves) lift WITH the lifter, he/she stands up with their help and the
  group walks it into the hooks together (46:58-47:02, 58:32-58:39, 68:42-68:46). Bails: spotters take it at the
  bottom almost immediately (63:41-63:43, 71:35.5-71:37). No dumps, no dropped bars.
- Reactions to misses: calm shrug + rueful smile + hug (W, 47:03-47:07); blank face, eyes down, walk off alone
  (58:40-58:46); head tipped back staring at the ceiling, mouth open, grimace (68:47-68:49); frown and handshake (63:46-
  63:49); hand to the lower back (71:43-71:46). Nobody throws anything or shouts.

- Coach greeting happens immediately backstage: hugs (most), daps / fist bumps (51:42, 66:47.5), handshake-hugs,
  back pats, the coach rushing in for a bear hug (74:52.5, 78:41). Coaches help with the belt (43:00).

## 9. The meet
- Platform: one squat rack centre stage, red/yellow Eleiko plates, bar visibly bends at 430-485 kg (67:07-67:10).
- Spotters/loaders: 5 in black HOUSTON shirts around the bar during each lift (2 per side + 1 rear); 2 loaders per side
  slide plates and spin the collar between attempts (47:17-47:21, 56:51-57:00). Officials' table at the platform front
  (58:40).
- Broadcast: wide front shot -> side shot for the walk-up -> tight face/plate close-ups under the bar -> wide front for
  the rep -> close-up for the rack -> steadicam following the walk-off backstage -> a REPLAY from a side or low angle
  (8 s, "REPLAY" bug top-left) with a red-wipe or HOUSTON-logo stinger before and after almost every attempt.
- Graphics: lower-third with the lifter's name, attempts, rank; a top strip "AMERICAN SQUAT RECORD ATTEMPT" for
  record tries; full-screen standings after the session (79:45-80:00).
- Lighting: dark room, white top light on the platform, coloured washes (red, green, blue, purple, pink) on transitions,
  red wash on a no-lift, haze and single spotlights backstage (73:44-73:45).
- Crowd: in the dark, loud during the lift ([screaming] / [cheering] in captions on every grinder and record), chants a
  lifter's name before the 485 (77:34). Commentators at a desk with headsets (79:09-79:44).
- Pace: ~45-60 s per attempt, flight order by weight on the bar; women's round 3 then men's rounds 1-3.

## 10. Surprises / what a game would most likely get wrong
1. The celebration usually happens BACKSTAGE, not on the platform: rack, step back, walk off the back, THEN the roar /
   arms wide in front of the "GOOD LIFT" wall with a camera in their face (70:54, 72:38, 73:42, 74:52, 75:55).
2. The biggest lifts get the smallest reactions: 430 kg -> nothing (57:45); 485 kg world record -> a grin and a long
   hug, no arms raised (78:38-78:46). The loudest celebrations came from 93 kg men hitting PR third attempts on grinders.
3. The setup under the bar is LONG and still: 8-15 s bent forward with the head bowed before standing it up
   (69:20-69:35). A game that unracks in 1 s will look fake.
4. Misses are slow and assisted, not dramatic: a 1.5-2 s stall just above parallel, then the spotters stand it up WITH
   the lifter (46:58-47:02, 58:32-58:39). No dumping.
5. Lifters do NOT stand and stare at the lights; the LED wall flash (white = good, red = no) does the job and they are
   already walking (46:00, 73:39.5). Only depth-call victims glance at the side judge (60:03).
6. Right hand to the bar first, very wide grips, and a "hands up" pause in front of the bar before ducking under
   (50:11-50:16).
7. After racking, almost everyone folds forward over the bar with the head down for ~1 s (40:34, 66:44, 74:49).
8. Ammonia and coach face-to-face hype happen backstage only, and they are short (65:05-65:08).
9. Women's celebrations here were as big as the men's openers: the crouch + fists + headbang (41:53) and hands on the
   head (48:29).
10. The stage itself reacts: white flash / GOOD LIFT letters, red light and NO LIFT, purple for a record attempt; the
    broadcast always replays the lift from the side.
