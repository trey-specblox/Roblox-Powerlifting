# Meet Celebration Catalogue

The celebration list the animators build from, made from the full 1-frame-per-second study of real meet videos (13 note files, [[Meet Study]]). It replaces nothing: the older, sparser [[Celebration Catalogue]] (CEL-01 to CEL-71, from a 3-second-step study of 15 of the same videos) stays as it is. Where an entry here is the same moment as an old CEL entry, it says so and keeps whichever breakdown is more detailed; where the two studies disagree, both readings are given.

Celebrations are built as R15 keyframe animations of 2-12 s and unlocked from crates. Men's celebrations come first and get the fullest breakdowns.

---

## 1. How to read this

**IDs**
- **MC-001 to MC-128**: men's celebrations, energy 5 first, then 4, 3, 2, 1.
- **MC-201 to MC-249**: women's celebrations, same order.
- **MT-01 to MT-18**: coach, team and crowd moves (the other people in the scene).
- **MH-01 to MH-36**: pre-lift hype and walk-on moves that could also be unlockable.
- **MB-01 to MB-30**: recurring building blocks (the common moves, with a how-to).

**Energy (1-5)**
- 1: almost nothing (a grin, a nod, a handshake).
- 2: one small gesture while walking (a fist, a wave, a point).
- 3: one clear big shape or two small beats (arms wide, V arms, a roar).
- 4: several big beats, the whole upper body, or a crouch roar.
- 5: the whole body: jumps, floor work, running, multi-beat roars, 5-15 s.

**Situation tags:** opener / 2nd / PR / last attempt / record (national or world) / win (takes the lead, a medal or the title) / grinder / after a miss (the lifter's previous attempt or this one was missed) / backstage.

**Where:** platform (on or right behind the bar) / walk-off (moving across the stage) / exit (at the stage edge, stairs or wings, usually with the coach) / backstage (behind the wall, in front of a backstage camera).

**Lights:** "before the lights" (started before the decision showed), "waited" (stood and looked for the decision, then celebrated), "after" (the lights were already up when it started), "red after" (celebrated, then got a no-lift).

**Times:** each move list starts at 0.0. The video time of 0.0 is given in brackets, so `0.0 (= 20:48)` means 0.0 is video time 20:48. L and R are the lifter's own left and right.

**Seen:** how many separate moments in the 13 note files show that move pattern. The building-block counts are in section 5.

**Notes file:** A1-A6 = Avancus Houston 2026 ([[Meet notes A1]] to [[Meet notes A6]]); S1, S2 = Sheffield 2026 single-lifter cuts ([[Meet notes S1]], [[Meet notes S2]]); C1-C4 = IPF Worlds, Sheffield 2024/2025 and highlight reels ([[Meet notes C1]] to [[Meet notes C4]]); X1 = misses, fails and the belt flick ([[Meet notes X1]]).

**R15 rules** (from [[Celebration Catalogue]], still true): the rig has no face and no fingers, so roars, tears and shock are carried by the body (head back or jutting, chest out, shoulders up, rigid arms; shoulders heaving for crying; stillness for shock). A pointed finger reads only from a straight arm with the hand angled in line. Kneels, sits, lie-downs and jumps move the LowerTorso through the Root joint; check them on the smallest and biggest builds. After a deadlift the bar is on the floor in front of the lifter.

**What the full study adds to the old picture**
- **Most good lifts get nothing.** Openers and second attempts are walked off. A 430 kg squat (eu_9nPA3zRs 57:45), a 303 kg open world-record deadlift (c5oY-m6ypNk 7:08) and a 275 kg bench PR (eu_9nPA3zRs 134:04) got no reaction at all. The big celebrations are on PRs, records, grinders, last attempts and wins.
- **Most big celebrations start before the lights.** The lifter knows. Waiting for the lights happens on close lifts (2-1 calls, grinders) and is a separate, readable beat. At Sheffield 2026 a 2-1 means a stone face, because the jury can overturn it (opYQsnkV1Vs 3:09).
- **They are done walking.** Rack, step back, then the celebration while walking toward the front camera, the stage edge or the exit, and it ends in a hug. At Houston the biggest roars happen backstage in front of the GOOD LIFT wall, with a steadicam in the lifter's face.
- **Men roar down and forward as often as up.** The half-squat roar with fists low by the hips (MB-02) is the single most common big men's move.
- **Lifters repeat a personal signature** every attempt (wings, double fists beside the head, bent-arm fist, salute, belt lift, the walk-on T). Give every lifter one equipped signature.
- **The venue celebrates too.** LED walls flash NEW WORLD RECORD, the room washes green, spark fountains fire. Several calm record-setters let the wall do the work (MC-116).

### MC-001 · World-record lockout roar, V hop, wings, thumbs up
- **Source:** lNOrz4CVNMY 20:48-20:59, front replay 21:01-21:11 ([[Meet notes C1]]). **Same moment as CEL-03.**
- **Situation:** record, 2nd deadlift, 383 kg open world record (sumo); the -83 kg crowd favourite (HUN, green singlet). Middle build.
- **Where:** platform, then backstage board. **Lights:** at the lockout ("nails it, three white lights"); the roar starts before "down".
- **Moves** (0.0 = 20:48, at lockout):
  - 0.0-2.25 holds the lockout ~2 s and ROARS while holding it: head thrown back, face to the ceiling, mouth wide, shoulders back, arms straight on the bar.
  - 2.5 starts lowering; 2.75 drops the bar fast to the knees, still holding it; 3.0-3.25 bar lands, he stays down in a deep sumo squat holding the bar, head up, roaring and grinning straight at the camera.
  - 3.5 lets go and SPRINGS UP; 3.75 both arms flung straight up in a big V, hands open, head back, knees bent as if landing from a small jump.
  - 4.0-4.25 arms come down and out wide, body twists to his right, face to the sky, eyes closed. 4.5-4.8 walks to the back, arms loose and swinging, a loader running past him to the bar.
  - [4.9-6.8 the edit cuts to the crowd, see MT-16.]
  - 7.0 at the backstage board: walks with BOTH ARMS SPREAD WIDE (his "wings"), palms open, grinning; 7.3 arms swing; 7.5 stops facing the camera, closed-mouth smile.
  - 7.9 both hands point back at his own chest ("that was me"); 8.2 BOTH THUMBS UP held high, elbows bent at shoulder height, forearms vertical; 8.4 swings both arms across above his head (a little dance); 8.6 turns away, arms swinging out; 9.0-10.5 walks to a backstage table (chalk/water).
  - Old CEL-03 reading of the same walk-off (B helper): arms flung wide and up ~45 deg with the torso leaning back ~15 deg and a step back (1.8), drifting down while he shouts into the stands, then at the exit both hands to the belt buckle and a full DOUBLE-BICEPS flex facing the crowd (6.0-6.3). Use either ending; the double biceps reads better at a distance, the thumbs up better in close-up.
- **Length:** ~10.5 s. **Energy:** 5. **Seen:** once (his wings pose 3 more times, MC-036, MC-054).
- **Crowd / coach / camera:** a whole stand block rises, arms up, clapping overhead, a woman with both palms on her head, phones up. No coach hug: he celebrates alone with the crowd. Front close camera, crowd cutaway, backstage board camera.
- **Animation notes:** the roar DURING the lockout hold (head back while still holding the bar) is unique and very readable; keep it 2 s. The V is a small hop, so it works on heavy builds if the hop is cut. The R15 hand has no thumb: use fists with forearms vertical, or a thumb hand later. Exaggerate the twist and the face-to-sky at 4.0.
