The player's Squat Control stat and the calculated lift difficulty will affect the minigame.

Higher difficulty should make the minigame harder.

Higher Squat Control should make the minigame easier.

Possible variables include:

- Movement speed
- Target/safe-zone size
- Stability
- Reaction time
