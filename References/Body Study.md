## Proportions (all builds)
- The classic Roblox skeleton: a full-size block head, a long torso, short legs, arms hanging at the torso's sides.
  The head is about half the shoulders' width on a fit body.
- **The head never sits on a visible neck.** The torso's top wraps the bottom of the head: on a big body the traps
  climb up the head's sides; on a fat body the round shoulders meet the head directly.
- Hands are big and chunky (wider than the wrist), with real fingers, knuckles and a thumb. They grip the bar with
  fingers wrapped round it.
- Feet are plain rounded shoes.

## Muscular (the "inflated" look)
- Every muscle is its own smooth, glossy, rounded shape. Where two meet there is a clean crease (pec over abs, delt
  over chest, biceps against forearm). No skin detail, no veins.
- Traps: huge slopes from the shoulder caps up the sides of the head. From behind, a diamond of trap down the
  upper back.
- Delts: big round caps sitting higher and wider than the chest, creased off from the chest and the arm.
- Pecs: two wide rounded slabs with a deep overhanging lower edge and a centre groove.
- Abs: 3 rows of 2 blocky pillows with deep grooves; obliques on the sides.
- Lats: flare out under the arms (a wide V from behind); a groove down the spine.
- Arms: biceps a ball in front, triceps a horseshoe behind; forearms thick at the elbow, tapering to the wrist.
- Hips stay narrow; glutes are round.
- Thighs: very thick, with a teardrop bulge above the inner knee and a sweep on the outside. Calves are a diamond.
- Clothes are tight shells over the muscles. Sleeves and shorts end in thick white cuffs (a fabric band); shorts have a
  doubled white waistband with drawstrings.

## Heavy
- Smooth and round with almost no definition. Round sloped shoulders meet the head.
- Soft chest; the belly sticks out in front and sits low (clear in side view), wider at the sides.
- Arms are smooth tubes, slightly thicker at the top. Legs are thick and short.

## How the body changes (the progression video)
- **Each muscle group grows from its own stat** (chest, back, arms, legs): a player who trains arms has big arms.
- **Growth is steady and continuous**: the silhouette swells a little at a time; the numbers grow much faster than the
  body (thousands to millions), so the body tracks something like the log of the stat. Early gains show fast;
  later ones keep showing, slower.
- At the top the body is far wider than it is tall, but the head stays its normal size.
- A reset ("Ascension") shrinks the body back to small and the climb starts again.

## What it means for our game (Claude's reading, for the user to decide)
- Our three lifts map onto muscle groups: Squat -> thighs and glutes, Bench -> chest, delts and triceps,
  Deadlift -> back, traps, forearms and hamstrings. A lifter's body could show which lift they are best at.
- Continuous growth suits Roblox: each body part can be made thicker in code (part width and depth) without
  touching its length or joints, so the animations still fit. A few base shapes (skinny, heavy, muscular) give the
  character; per-part thickness gives the steady climb.
- Weight classes stay the limit (GAME_DESIGN §38.1): growth fills out the class, it never makes a 59 kg lifter look
  heavier than 59 kg.
