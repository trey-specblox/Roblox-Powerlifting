## 7. Setup and lift mechanics

**Squat**
- Grip wide (hands well outside the shoulders), bar on the upper back (8lBTmPY5_Jc 1:44-1:51).
- Unrack: stand up from the head-down position, 2-3 steps back (OzNSQQoeHWo 0:25-0:27).
- Reps: opener ~1 s down / ~1-1.5 s up (8lBTmPY5_Jc 0:36.5-0:39.5; OzNSQQoeHWo 0:28-0:31). Max/grinder: ~1 s down,
  3-4 s up with a slow middle (8lBTmPY5_Jc 2:02-2:07; YMrXwUrTpkw 1:55.5-2:00, 335 kg record).
- Spotters: 3-5 big men in black; for heavy attempts they shadow the lifter in a half crouch with hands cupped
  under the bar ends through the whole rep (YMrXwUrTpkw 1:55-2:00), then all grab the bar to rack it.
- Rack: walk forward 2-3 steps into the uprights; spotters' hands on the bar.

**Bench**
- Setup (8lBTmPY5_Jc 3:42.5-3:49; side view): lie down, grab the bar wide, LIFT THE HIPS UP into a bridge with the
  feet planted wide, slide the upper back up under the bar, then lower the hips with a big arch, feet pulled back
  toward the head with heels up. Overhead view (OzNSQQoeHWo 2:53-2:57; YMrXwUrTpkw 2:21-2:25): feet very wide and
  flat, knees bent ~90.
- Handoff from the centre spotter at the head end.
- Rep: ~1-1.5 s down to the lower chest, a short pause, ~1-1.5 s press (YMrXwUrTpkw 2:28-2:34; 8lBTmPY5_Jc
  replay 4:11-4:17). Elbows ~45-60 deg from the body; forearms vertical at the bottom.
- After the rack: sits up straddling the bench (back to the head-end camera), stands, walks forward and checks the
  lights.

**Deadlift**
- All three lifters studied here pull SUMO: very wide stance (feet nearly at the plates), toes out ~45 deg, hands
  inside the knees, narrow grip; knees pushed out (8lBTmPY5_Jc 4:42-4:47; OzNSQQoeHWo 4:41-4:43;
  YMrXwUrTpkw 5:37-5:43).
- Setup: hips start high, then sink while the chest comes up (4:46-4:47 in 8lBTmPY5_Jc).
- Pull: easy ~1-1.5 s floor to lockout (8lBTmPY5_Jc 5:04-5:09; YMrXwUrTpkw 5:43-5:44); lockout held ~1-2 s;
  lowered with control in ~1 s. Hard max: bar breaks the floor and STALLS just below the knee for 8-10 s with hips
  high, lifter still pulling (8lBTmPY5_Jc 7:12-7:22, a miss).
- After the lift the spotters immediately come in from both ends and start stripping plates while the lifter is
  still on the platform (OzNSQQoeHWo 6:05; YMrXwUrTpkw 5:48).
