### C1. "Wingspan" (arms flung out wide, chest proud) -- HUN lifter's signature move
- Source: lNOrz4CVNMY 6:26.0-6:28.0 (after a 300 kg 3rd squat that was a 6 s grinder; crowd screaming; commentator
  "OH MY GOSH... DON'T COUNT HIM OUT"), and again 17:31.8-17:33.6 (after an easy 355 kg 2nd deadlift). It is his
  signature, so build it as one reusable move.

  - 0.6 s (6:26.7): right arm extends up-and-out at ~45 deg above horizontal, left hand still at the belt.
  - 0.9 s (6:27.0): BOTH arms flung out wide: right arm up-out ~30 deg above horizontal, left arm straight out to the
    side at shoulder height, palms open facing forward/down, fingers spread; head tips back slightly, mouth open in a

## 7. Setup and lift mechanics

SQUAT (IPF style, 5 spotters):
- Grip: wide, hands well outside the shoulders; elbows point down and back.
- Unrack: stands up under the bar, 2-3 steps back (walkout ~1.5-3 s), feet about shoulder width or a bit wider, toes
  slightly out; then stands still and braced 3-5 s waiting for the "squat" command (2:25-2:29, 1:44.5-1:47).
- Easy rep (USA 1:47.5-1:52, front-left camera): descent ~1.5 s; bottom with the hip crease below the top of the
  knee, torso ~45 deg forward, knees forward over the toes and pushed out; ascent ~2 s; lockout; waits for "rack";
  2-3 steps forward into the rack (~1 s).
- Grinder (HUN 300, 6:15-6:22): out of the hole the hips rise first, torso tips to ~50 deg forward and the knees stop
  at ~110 deg (stall 6:19-6:19.5), then the hips drive forward and the chest comes up; ~6 s from bottom to lockout.
  Spotters crouch down with the lifter, open hands under the bar ends, not touching.
- Miss in the hole (0:34.5-0:38, 8:53-8:57): no upward movement for ~2-3 s, then the side and back spotters grab the
  bar and lift; the lifter stands with them and they walk it into the rack together.
BENCH:
- Set-up: feet planted, bridge the hips, walk the feet back under the bench toward the head, hips back down = big
  arch (9:16-9:22). Some arch very high with the feet far back (USA 11:04-11:10).
- Grip: full grip, thumb around, wide.
- Hand-off from the centre spotter; arms locked over the shoulders ~2 s waiting for "start".
- Easy rep (HUN 9:31-9:35): down ~1 s to the lower chest, elbows tucked ~45-60 deg, finishing below bench level;
  still on the chest ~0.5-1 s; press ~1.5 s with the bar drifting slightly back toward the face; lockout; wait for
  "rack"; rack.
- Hard rep (former champ, 10:05-10:10): slow press ~4 s. Failing grinder (HUN 11:41.5-11:52): bar stuck about 3/4
  of the way up, elbows ~140 deg, inching for ~9 s, then the spotters take it.
- After racking, lifters SIT STRAIGHT UP at once (10:11, 13:27.5), then stand.
DEADLIFT (mostly wide sumo stances here):
- Sumo set-up: very wide stance, toes out ~45 deg, shins touching the bar, hands inside the knees; hips sink, chest
  lifts, head comes up just before the pull (15:49.5-15:50.5).
- Easy pull: bar breaks the floor with the hips still low, mid-shin at 0.5 s, knee at 1 s, hips through at 1.5 s,
  lockout at ~2-2.5 s, shoulders back, head neutral (15:51-15:53.5). The world-record pull was even quicker
  (~1.5-2 s, 20:46.5-20:48).
- Lockout held still ~1.5 s until "down", then lowered under control in ~1 s with the hands on the bar
  (15:55.5-15:56).
- Failing pulls: the hips rise but the bar never leaves the floor (19:34-19:38, 21:48-21:57, 23:15-23:25, 24:09),
  or it reaches the knees, stops and drops back (25:02-25:05).
- Body types (all -83 kg): HUN lifter shortest and thickest; former champ tall and long-limbed (sumo, powdered
  thighs); USA champion stocky with a very big bench arch; GBR winner lean with long hair tied back.

- Squat stuck in the hole: spotters take it, lifter walks out with his head down (0:34.5-0:42, 8:56-9:00).
- Bench miss, subdued: lips pressed, eyes down, short low handshake with the centre spotter (11:24-11:26).
- Bench miss with PAIN: sits up, grabs the elbow/upper arm, winces, folds forward with the head down (12:16.5-12:19.5
  overhead, 13:27.5-13:33), walks off holding the forearm.
- "BELT RIP AND THROW" (USA, 19:38.5-19:42, after failing to break the bar from the floor; commentary: he throws his
  belt off in anger): 0.0 s lets go and rises; 0.5 s both hands at the buckle; 0.8-1.0 s rips the lever open; 1.3 s
  belt off in the right hand, already walking fast to the side; ~2.8 s flings the belt down/aside behind him; walks
  off without looking back. Energy 4 (anger).
- Gracious final miss: crowd applauds, lifter raises an open right hand to them and nods (23:30-23:31, see C7).
- Calm acceptance: lets go, stands, walks off head down (21:57-22:02); crowd faces with hands on heads (22:00-22:01).
- Exhausted miss: stays bent over the bar with both hands on it after it falls back (25:04-25:05).
