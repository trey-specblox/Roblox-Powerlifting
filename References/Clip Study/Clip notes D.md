### C1. Record jump-and-roar (big, full sequence)

Feet shoulder-width, knees soft. Facing the crowd side.
  - 3.5-5.0 (9:46-9:47.5) walks toward the front camera holding the same open-arms pose, mouth wide, eyebrows up (a "can you believe it" face). Lights come on white at 9:46.
  - 5.5 (9:48) arms drop; walks a few steps; 6.0 looks up at the LED wall where "RECORD" flashes (9:49.5-9:50.5); face calm for ~2 s, as if reading it.
  - 8.5 (9:51) both arms rise; 9.0 (9:51.5) both hands land on top of his head (palms on the crown, elbows out wide), mouth open: disbelief.
  - 9.5 (9:52) knees start to bend with hands still on the head; 10.0 (9:52.5) folds forward at the hips, hands over the back of the head, face down (second camera, side).
  - 10.5 (9:53) straightens, arms come forward; 11.0 (9:53.5) arms out in front at chest height, elbows bent, mouth wide (roar), small hop.
  - 11.3 (9:53.8) skipping step, arms swing forward; 11.8 (9:54.3) right knee driven up high, fists at chest.

## 7. Setup and lift mechanics
### Squat
- Monolift-style stands, but lifters still walk the bar out 2-3 steps (side shots); 5-6 loaders/spotters around (two at the bar ends with hands near the sleeves, more behind).
- Grip: wide, hands well outside the shoulders (~1.5-1.8x shoulder width), thumbs around, elbows pointing down/back (up-LEMKGRA8 3:59; c6JJZD1W-QA 0:25).
- Under the bar: head ducked, back slightly rounded, knees bent ~20-30 deg, looking at the floor (up-LEMKGRA8 4:07; c6JJZD1W-QA 0:33); then head and chest come up, elbows drive back, knees ~25 deg (4:11; 0:36.5); stands it up.
- Stance: shoulder width or a little wider, toes slightly out, flat squat shoes (white on Lifter C).
- Tempo (good lifts): braced 2-4 s, descent ~1.5 s, depth clearly below parallel (c6JJZD1W-QA 3:09-3:10 replay: hips below the knees, knees pushed out wide, torso ~45 deg), ascent ~1.5 s on easy lifts, 2.5-3 s on hard thirds (2:44-2:46).
- Failed squat (up-LEMKGRA8 4:25-4:29.5): stays in the hole ~3 s, hips rise first so the torso folds forward to ~45 deg and lower, loaders bend and wrap their arms around the bar and take it.
### Bench
- Grip wide, forearms vertical at the bottom (up-LEMKGRA8 9:34), wrist wraps, thumbs around the bar, eyes directly under the bar (7:34-7:43 close-up).
- Arch: large, feet tucked back toward the head (legs-up setup) or planted wide and back (c6JJZD1W-QA 5:15.5).
- Hand-off: a centre spotter straddles the head end and lifts the bar out with the lifter (up-LEMKGRA8 5:43); two side spotters.
- Tempo: lowering ~1-1.5 s, pause on the chest ~1 s, press ~1.5 s easy, ~2.5 s hard (c6JJZD1W-QA 5:40-5:42).
- On a miss Lifter A hops off immediately and walks away fast (up-LEMKGRA8 9:02.5).
### Deadlift
- Conventional (Lifter A, up-LEMKGRA8 12:06.5-12:11.5): hips high while gripping, then hips drop to just above knee height, back flat, shoulders over the bar, eyes forward-down; bar leaves the floor 12:09, passes the knees 12:10, lockout 12:10.5-12:11 (~1.5-2 s pull on the opener); lockout = standing tall, shoulders back, knees straight; holds ~1 s for "down"; lowers with hands on the bar, controlled, ~1.5 s.
- Grinder (up-LEMKGRA8 15:35-15:42): bar stalls just above the knees for 6-7 s, knees still bent, torso ~45 deg, shoulders behind the bar, straining, then lowered without lockout.
- Sumo (Lifter B 12:45; Lifter C 7:03-7:13): feet very wide (toes near the plates), toes turned out ~45 deg, knees pushed out, arms straight down inside the knees, shoulder-width grip, hips low, chest up. Pull 1-2 s on easy lifts (c6JJZD1W-QA 7:19-7:20), ~5 s on the record third (9:36-9:42, slow past the knees).
- Body types: all three lifters in batch D are 66 kg (small, compact). Lifter A lean and tall for the class; Lifter C stocky and thick; Lifter B compact. No heavyweights in this batch.
