# Clip Study

The game's reference study of real powerlifting meets.

On **2026-11-11**, six helpers (batches A-F) watched **15 YouTube videos** of real meets from start to end: IPF Worlds 2026, IPF Junior Worlds 2026, Sheffield 2024-2026 and US record shows. They studied them frame by frame: overview grids every 3 s, then 0.25-1 s close-ups. **The study was muted, so no sound was heard.** Every sound note is inferred from open mouths, claps and contact. Batches B, C and D read some auto-captions (commentary) for context.

Each helper wrote notes an animator can build from without seeing the video: what each body part does, in order, with timings.

- **Celebrations** (the main goal) are merged into one list: [[Celebration Catalogue]] (71 entries, with rarities, a first build set and the default).
- The raw notes: [[Clip notes A]], [[Clip notes B]], [[Clip notes C]], [[Clip notes D]], [[Clip notes E]], [[Clip notes F]].

## Topic map

### Celebrations after a good lift
All merged in [[Celebration Catalogue]].
- **How often:** most good lifts get little or nothing. The big multi-beat celebrations come on last attempts, wins, PRs and records.
- **Beat order:** bar down, a still beat, one big move, the walk off, then the coach.
- **Lights:** many lifters wait for the referee lights before they explode.
- **Body type:** heavyweights plant their feet; light lifters jump, jog and squat down.
- **Signatures:** lifters repeat personal signature moves.

### Coach rituals
Best in [[Clip notes D]], [[Clip notes E]], [[Clip notes F]] and [[Clip notes A]] (section 3 in each).
- **Trap grip and ear talk** is the most common coach move. The coach stands directly behind the lifter, kneads both traps at about 1 squeeze per second and talks into the right ear, for 6-20 s before every attempt. Then he pushes the lifter off by the upper back, or walks him to the curtain with both hands on his shoulders (c6JJZD1W-QA 0:02-0:12 and every attempt, [[Clip notes D]]; all three lifters in [[Clip notes E]]).
- **Headphones:** the coach presses both palms over the lifter's headphones to calm her, and lifting them off is the "go" signal; she claps her chalky hands at once (8lBTmPY5_Jc 0:07, 4:35, [[Clip notes F]]).
- **Scream into the ear from behind,** and the lifter screams back (3i4jvmDrtdY 3:01, [[Clip notes F]]).
- **Face-to-face shout** with a grip on the shoulders, then 2-3 hard slaps on the upper arms (gfp-IdEaQNc 11:31, [[Clip notes A]]; up-LEMKGRA8 16:12, [[Clip notes D]]).
- **Neck cup with heads together** (jfMBhKh4ab4 4:52); the coach cinching the lever belt (9FQM0Z6_s8Q 3:02), both [[Clip notes A]].
- **During the lift:** IPF coaches stand still with arms folded, a fist at the mouth ([[Clip notes B]]). Others crouch at the exit ([[Clip notes C]]), kneel and shout with cupped hands at a US nationals ([[Clip notes A]]), or run a hands-to-mouth, point, "UP!" loop ([[Clip notes F]] F19).
- **After the lift:** coaches **wait at the exit**, never on the platform. Hugs, clasps, low fives, a hand on the chest, a lift and spin: see COACH-01 to COACH-13 in [[Celebration Catalogue]].

### Before the platform (backstage)
Best in [[Clip notes D]] and [[Clip notes E]], also [[Clip notes A]] and [[Clip notes F]] (section 4).
- **Sheffield warm-up room:** dark, low ceiling, purple/magenta LED strips, wooden doors, monitors with the live feed, and a chalk bowl on a stand. Lifters queue right behind the curtain.
- **Ammonia (smelling salts):** the handler holds the capsule, the lifter cups his hands round his nose, his head snaps back, and he shakes his head, holds his ears and yells (up-LEMKGRA8 0:11, 3:33-3:41, 12:24, [[Clip notes D]]).
- **Personal rituals:** a headband pulled down as a blindfold for 5-8 s, an ear pinch, hands over the ears ([[Clip notes E]]); big over-ear headphones with swaying to music (9FQM0Z6_s8Q 3:40, [[Clip notes A]]); winding wrist wraps (gfp-IdEaQNc 0:25, [[Clip notes A]]); a still stare into space.
- **Side-of-stage primer:** both arms clapped overhead (a chalk puff), head back, then a deep forward fold (up-LEMKGRA8 2:07, [[Clip notes D]]).

### Walk-ups
Best in [[Clip notes B]], [[Clip notes C]] and [[Clip notes D]] (section 5).
- **Timing:** about 3-6 s from the curtain to the bar. The 1:00 attempt clock is already running; squatters reach the bar with 45-50 s left ([[Clip notes B]]). Eyes on the bar, arms loose, a steady pace.
- **Gestures:**
  - one arm straight up for noise ([[Clip notes A]], [[Clip notes D]], [[Clip notes E]]);
  - airplane arms on the walk-in ([[Clip notes E]]);
  - "raise the roof" (oGfa7HoDrrA 8:09-8:28, [[Clip notes C]]);
  - "lift the crowd", palms up, and a war crouch with the arms swung back ([[Clip notes B]]);
  - a fist bump with each loader (up-LEMKGRA8 6:32, [[Clip notes D]]).
- **The super-heavyweight walk:** a wide base, rocking from side to side, arms held away from the body (LxSRpjl_UGU 0:21, [[Clip notes E]]). Heavyweights climb the stage steps with heavy, deliberate steps ([[Clip notes C]]).

### Pre-lift rituals at the bar
Best in [[Clip notes C]], [[Clip notes B]] and [[Clip notes D]] (section 6).
- **Kneel under the squat bar:** hanging from the bar with the head bowed between straight arms, for ~1.5-2 s (lNOrz4CVNMY 2:12.5, [[Clip notes B]]).
- **Grip one hand at a time:** each arm slides out along the bar nearly straight, palm forward, then the elbows drop and the head ducks under ([[Clip notes B]]). A head-down settle under the bar for ~4 s ([[Clip notes F]]).
- **Belt, arm swings, chalk clap:** the belt is tightened, the thighs slapped, an X arm swing across the chest, a clap overhead that throws a chalk cloud, the arms flung out, then straight to the grip (oGfa7HoDrrA 3:05-3:17, [[Clip notes C]]).
- **Turn your back to the bar, then head back and breathe** ([[Clip notes C]] YLMRf70ALKo 12:31; [[Clip notes F]] OzNSQQoeHWo 5:32).
- **Wings and V** ([[Clip notes C]]); slapping your own traps ([[Clip notes A]] 9FQM0Z6_s8Q 6:41.5).
- **Resets:** grip, stand back up, breathe, go again ([[Clip notes A]], [[Clip notes D]], [[Clip notes F]]). Sumo wedges where the hips sink for 8-20 s ([[Clip notes D]]).

### Setup and lift mechanics
**Squat:** best in [[Clip notes B]], [[Clip notes E]] and [[Clip notes D]] (section 7).
- **Setup:** a wide grip (super-heavyweights hold the bar almost at the collars). Duck under, unrack, take 2-3 steps back, then stand braced 2-15 s waiting for the "squat" command.
- **Easy rep:** descent ~1.2-3 s, the hip crease below the knee, the torso ~40-45 deg forward with the knees out. Ascent ~1.5-2 s.
- **Grinders:** 3.5-6 s. The hips rise first, the torso tips to ~50 deg and the knees stall at ~110 deg, then the hips drive forward (lNOrz4CVNMY 6:15-6:22, [[Clip notes B]]).
- **Super-heavyweight:** the belly presses onto the thighs at the bottom ([[Clip notes E]]).
- **Spotters:** 3-5 spotters crouch with the lifter, hands hovering 5-10 cm from the bar ([[Clip notes C]]).

**Bench:** best in [[Clip notes B]], [[Clip notes D]] and [[Clip notes F]].
- **Setups:**
  - bridge the hips and walk the feet back toward the head (lNOrz4CVNMY 9:16, [[Clip notes B]]);
  - the legs-up setup: legs swung vertical, then planted far back (up-LEMKGRA8 6:39.5, [[Clip notes D]]);
  - pull yourself up the bench by the bar;
  - the super-heavyweight rocks back onto the bench with momentum (LxSRpjl_UGU 3:20, [[Clip notes E]]).
- **Grip:** wide, with the forearms vertical at the bottom. The hand-off comes from a centre spotter who stays over the head.
- **Tempo:** down 1-2 s, a 0.5-1 s pause, press 1.5-2 s easy. Grinders run 3-9 s, with the bar stuck about 3/4 of the way up. After racking, lifters sit straight up.

**Deadlift:** best in [[Clip notes B]] (sumo timing), [[Clip notes D]] (a clean conventional side view, up-LEMKGRA8 11:56-12:14), [[Clip notes C]] and [[Clip notes A]].
- **Sumo:** a very wide stance with the toes out ~45 deg, hands inside the knees. The hips start high, then sink as the chest lifts.
- **Easy pulls:** 1-2.5 s floor to lockout. Grinders: 5-10 s.
- **Lockout:** held 1-5 s waiting for "down", then lowered under control in ~1 s.
- **Fails:** either the bar never leaves the floor, or it stalls at the knee.
- **Loaders** rush in the moment the bar touches down.

### Misses and reactions
Best in [[Clip notes B]] and [[Clip notes D]] (section 8).
- **Angry:** belt rip and throw (lNOrz4CVNMY 19:38.5).
- **Pain:** grabbing the elbow after a bench.
- **Gracious:** a wave to the crowd, or shaking the referees' hands. The proud-miss farewell is the longest version (up-LEMKGRA8 15:45.8-16:03).
- **Light-hearted:** a laugh-off.
- **Calm:** a head-down walk-off.
- **Consoling hug** at the exit after a world-record miss ([[Clip notes C]]).
- **Looking back at the lights** after celebrating too early ([[Clip notes D]]).
- The highlight reels in [[Clip notes A]] show no misses.

### Camera and presentation
Best in [[Clip notes A]], [[Clip notes D]], [[Clip notes F]] and [[Clip notes B]] (section 9).
- **Two looks:**
  - **IPF Worlds** is bright, flat TV light: a white world-map backdrop on a blue carpet, with no light change even for a world record ([[Clip notes B]]).
  - **Sheffield** is dark: hot-pink (2025) or red (2024) giant SHF letter walls, haze, white backlights, and low close cameras ([[Clip notes D]], [[Clip notes F]]). The F helper: "darkness + one bright platform + coloured back wall + haze + low, close cameras on faces at the exit."
- **Green light on a good lift:** the whole room or stage washes **GREEN** when the white lights come (up-LEMKGRA8 12:17, c6JJZD1W-QA many times, [[Clip notes D]]; US shows at gfp-IdEaQNc 1:49-2:31, [[Clip notes A]]).
- **Video walls on records:** the walls flip to NEW / WORLD RECORD (9FQM0Z6_s8Q, [[Clip notes A]]; Mw95YfGN7Vg, [[Clip notes E]]; c6JJZD1W-QA 9:49.5, [[Clip notes D]]). The French record stage fires CO2 jets and cold sparks at lockout (gfp-IdEaQNc 3:10, [[Clip notes A]]).
- **Cameras:**
  - a wide front shot and a front-left 3/4 main camera;
  - an overhead top-down camera for the bench and deadlift;
  - a low camera at the bench foot end;
  - a ground-level camera with a plate in the foreground for lifters on the floor;
  - **a low handheld at the stage exit that catches every celebration**;
  - a steadicam following from backstage through the curtain;
  - slow-motion front replays with a REPLAY tag;
  - 1-3 s crowd cutaways.
- **Graphics:** a lower third with three light dots and the 1:00 clock; "IF SUCCESSFUL" (rank change); a "FORECAST" leaderboard; "CURRENT WORLD RECORD"; a full-screen lifter card; "ATTEMPT CHANGE".

### Big-body vs light-body movement
Best in [[Clip notes C]] ("How celebrations change with body type and stakes") and [[Clip notes E]].
- **Heavyweights (-120 kg):** the feet stay planted, with no jumps, squats or jogging; celebrations are upper body only plus a slow walk. They look winded after a max (hands on hips, unbuckling the belt) and move the big emotion to the coach hug. But some big lifters do lower themselves to the floor on purpose (CEL-06, CEL-12 in [[Celebration Catalogue]]).
- **Super-heavyweights:** a wide-based rocking walk, arms held off the body, the whole torso turning rather than just the head; they push up off the knees to stand; the rock-back bench lie-down ([[Clip notes E]]).
- **Light lifters** (the -59 kg juniors, -47 to -69 kg women, lean -74 kg men): hops off the platform edge, jogs with heel kicks, bounces, deep squats, tuck jumps, and a gesture on almost every good lift ([[Clip notes C]], [[Clip notes A]]).
- **All -66 kg** in [[Clip notes D]]; **all -83 kg** in [[Clip notes B]] (shortest and thickest vs. tall and long-limbed).
