# 3. Coach rituals

- Behind-the-back trap massage (all three lifters; the most common coach move): coach stands directly behind the lifter
  and digs both thumbs into the upper traps at the base of the neck, fingers over the front of the shoulders, kneading
  in small circles or quick squeezes, sometimes a small shake (Mw95YfGN7Vg 1:00-1:05, 5:24-5:26; FkQMMNSD6HI 0:02-0:08,
  0:38-0:43, 5:14-5:36 (~20 s)). The lifter stands still, head bowed, eyes down.
- Talking into the ear: coach at the lifter's shoulder, mouth near his ear, one hand on the upper back or upper arm
  (Mw95YfGN7Vg 2:03.5, 4:11-4:13).
- Face-to-face pep talk: coach squares up ~30 cm from the lifter's face and talks hard, hand flat on the lifter's chest
  or belt (LxSRpjl_UGU 7:17-7:27).
- Smelling salts: a handler holds a small white object under the lifter's nose ~2 s, the lifter's head tilts back
  slightly, then it is pulled away (Mw95YfGN7Vg 7:15-7:18; LxSRpjl_UGU 7:19-7:21). Probably ammonia; too small to be sure.
- Coach walks right behind the lifter to the tunnel and waits in the wing (Mw95YfGN7Vg 6:39-6:41).
- Coach straightens the lifter's singlet strap/belt before he goes (LxSRpjl_UGU 7:33).
- Coach's forearm passes across the lifter's face/head (head grab or wiping him; unclear) (Mw95YfGN7Vg 4:15).
- After the lift: coach waits crouched at the side of the stage to clasp hands (Mw95YfGN7Vg 3:03).

# 7. Setup and lift mechanics

## Squat
- Grip wide on all three, hands well outside the shoulders; super-heavyweight's hands almost at the collars.
- Grip first while standing in front of the bar, then dip the head under (chin down), bar high on the traps. Unrack
  with a small knee extension, 2-3 short steps back, set feet: wide stance (lifter 1 ~1.7x shoulder width, toes out
  ~30 deg; lifter 2 a bit narrower; lifter 3 just outside the shoulders).
- Elbows back/up, chest up, eyes forward. Brace pause ~1 s before descending (waiting for "squat").
- Descent ~1.2-1.5 s (lifter 2's side view runs real time). Bottom: hip crease below the top of the knee, torso ~40-45
  deg forward, knees pushed out over the toes. Super-heavyweight: belly presses onto the thighs at the bottom.
- Ascent ~2 s on an easy rep. A GRINDER (FkQMMNSD6HI 2:03-2:06.5) takes ~3.5 s with a slow zone just above parallel
  where the bar barely moves, torso tipping slightly forward, then hips and chest rise together.
- After lockout: waits for "rack", walks the bar forward into the hooks; the two side spotters close in.
- Mw95YfGN7Vg 3:10-3:18: slow front replay of a world-record squat; clearest view of knees-out and torso angle.

## Bench
- FkQMMNSD6HI 2:42-2:50 (camera at the foot end): sits, lies back under the bar, plants the feet; WIDE grip (hands near
  the rings); then PULLS HIMSELF UP OFF THE BENCH by the bar (head and upper back lifted) to squeeze the shoulder
  blades and set the arch, lowers back with the chest high; elbows slightly flared; handoff.
- FkQMMNSD6HI 4:00-4:14 (bench 3, foot-end camera): lift-off 4:00-4:02, arms locked; lowers ~1 s (4:03); pause on the
  chest 4:04-4:05; press; GRIND 4:06-4:11 with elbows ~90 deg and the bar barely moving ~4 s; lockout 4:12-4:13; rack;
  three reds ("some up and down": the bar sank during the press).
- The main Sheffield bench camera sits by the head end, low; the plates hide most of the body in Mw95YfGN7Vg.

## Deadlift (all three conventional)
- Shins near the bar, feet ~hip width; hinge, grip just outside the shins (grip type not readable); hips start HIGH
  (almost stiff-legged) for ~0.5-1 s, then DROP into the start position (back flat, shoulders over the bar, head
  neutral), then pull.
- Easy pull (Mw95YfGN7Vg 7:43-7:45, FkQMMNSD6HI 4:39.5-4:41): floor to lockout ~1.2-1.5 s; hips rise a little first,
  bar past the knees at ~0.5 s, lockout tall with shoulders back; holds ~1 s for "down"; lowers by hinging (~1 s).
- Hard pull (Mw95YfGN7Vg 8:31-8:35, 342.5 kg, red): breaks the floor slowly, crawls past the knees, ~3-4 s to lockout;
  in the slow replay (8:54.5) the bar BENDS with the plates lagging at mid-thigh.
- Failed pull (LxSRpjl_UGU 8:01-8:03, 411.5 kg): hips high, bar crawls to about the knees, stalls, goes back down.
- Super-heavyweight: wide-legged walk to the bar, hands on thighs while staring at it, hips set high (belly in the way).
