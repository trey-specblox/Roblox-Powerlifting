### Patterns across all celebrations (for the animator)
- **Order of beats.** The order is almost always the same: bar down → 0.3-1 s of stillness or straightening (stunned) → one big move (arms up, roar, drop to the floor) → walk off → coach hug. The first 0.5 s is never a celebration: lifters finish the lift and set the bar down first.
- **Most common shapes, by count in batch A:** dropping to the knees or face to the floor (9); both hands on the head in disbelief (5); arms spread wide (5); a crouched roar with fists clenched low (5); a single fist or finger to the sky (5); a V with both arms up (5); belt pulled off and raised overhead (3); a coach hug, lift or jump-hug (8+).
- **Two-beat celebrations feel the best:** quiet, then explosive (4:28 in jfMBhKh4ab4, bow then roar), or explosive, then quiet (1:55 in jfMBhKh4ab4, leap then bow).
- **Emotion is staged in the space.** Lifters walk **toward the exit and the coach** (screen side) while celebrating, rarely toward the judges. At Sheffield they celebrate along the record wall on the way out.
- **Records change the room.** The video wall or back wall flips to NEW / WORLD RECORD and the stage light changes (green, colour wash). Lifters often look up at it, which is a natural camera cue.

## 3. Coach rituals

## 6. Pre-lift rituals at the bar
- **Head bowed, looking at the bar**, breathing, sometimes with a small smile before bending (jfMBhKh4ab4 0:35, 1:18-1:27).
- **Big breath to the ceiling.** Head tipped back, chest lifting, forceful exhale with mouth open, eyes closed, hands on hips, then down to the bar (jfMBhKh4ab4 4:02-4:12).

## 7. Setup and lift mechanics
**Squat** (jfMBhKh4ab4 5:05-5:28; gfp-IdEaQNc 0:03-0:15, 0:47-1:04, 1:29-1:48, 2:11-2:28, 2:59-3:12, 3:26-3:47, 4:17-4:24)
- **Spotters.** Usually 3 (one at each side, one behind). Sheffield and the record shows use 5. The back spotter's hands hover under the bar and follow it down. Side spotters crouch with hands under the sleeves.
- **Unrack and walkout.** Short: 1-3 steps back. He then stands set for 2-15 s waiting for the command.
- **Descent.** About 2-3 s, controlled. Depth is deep, with the hip crease clearly below the knee. The torso is about 45 deg forward at the bottom on a low bar. Elbows are back and the grip is wide.
- **Ascent.** Easy reps take about 2 s. Grinders take 4-5 s and slow down at mid-shin to mid-thigh (gfp-IdEaQNc 3:41-3:46 is nearly stalled for about 3 s, with spotters' hands right under the bar). At lockout the mouth is often open, shouting.
- **Rack.** Side spotters grab the sleeves and walk the bar forward into the rack. Lifters often stay holding it until it is in.

**Bench** (gfp-IdEaQNc 4:37-4:54, 5:04-5:23, 5:45-6:20, 6:21-6:45, 7:06-7:22)
- **Grip and arch.** Wide grip, index fingers near the rings (seen from the overhead camera at 6:14-6:20). Shoulder blades pinched, big arch, feet tucked back. Handoff from a centre spotter behind the head.
- **Tempo.** Easy reps take about 1-2 s up. Grinders take 6-8 s (5:52-6:02), with the bar barely moving mid-way. The bar path goes straight up and slightly back toward the face.
- **After.** The lifter often lies on the bench for 3-4 s after racking (jfMBhKh4ab4 5:34-5:46), then sits up.

**Deadlift** (most clips)
- **Sumo** (jfMBhKh4ab4 lifters A, B, C, E and I; many IPF women). The stance is very wide, with feet near the plates and toes out about 45 deg. Hands grip inside the knees. Shins are vertical and knees pushed out over the toes (close-up at jfMBhKh4ab4 4:21-4:22). The torso is fairly upright.
- **Conventional** (jfMBhKh4ab4 D; gfp-IdEaQNc O; 9FQM0Z6_s8Q #9, #4 and #1). Hands just outside the legs, hips higher. The bar drags up the shins and thighs in a vertical line (low side view at gfp-IdEaQNc 9:34-9:40).
- **Tempo.** Easy pulls go from floor to lockout in about 1 s (jfMBhKh4ab4 0:36-0:37). Grinders take 5-8 s: jfMBhKh4ab4 7:25-7:35 breaks the floor slowly over about 3 s, crawls up the shins, passes the knees around 7:32 and locks out around 7:35; gfp-IdEaQNc 11:43-11:49. 9FQM0Z6_s8Q #10 and #5 are slow too.
- **Lockout.** Shoulders back, hips through. He holds 1-5 s waiting for the down command. Often the head is thrown back screaming at lockout (jfMBhKh4ab4 7:35.5-7:36.5).
- **Lowering.** Mostly controlled, about 0.5-1 s. Some let go at the top so the bar crashes (gfp-IdEaQNc 9:13.5).
- **Setup on a grinder.** Lifters often reset once or twice before the pull (see section 6).

**Body types seen:** very heavy super-heavyweights (jfMBhKh4ab4 F, gfp-IdEaQNc O and G) who move slowly and walk heavily; light women (-47 to -63 kg) who are quick and bouncy; lean -74 kg men who are explosive.
