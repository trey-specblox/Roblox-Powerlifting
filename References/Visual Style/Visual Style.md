# Visual Style — Reference

## Direction

**Realistic, bright and clean powerlifting gym.** *(Approved 2026-10-18; replaces the earlier "dark and gritty" direction, which the user rejected.)* It should feel like a real, well-kept powerlifting gym, not a dark hardcore dungeon and not a cartoon simulator.

Key traits to aim for:

- **Bright, clean, even lighting**, like the Roblox "Gym Build" example (reference 10)
- **One big open gym floor full of equipment**, not split into sections like Iron Built. Other rooms are cosmetic only
- Wall, floor and material feel taken from the Iron Built Gym tour (reference 1)
- Black rubber flooring
- **One station type everywhere** *(approved 2026-10-18)*: every training station is a **black ER combo rack with a bench**, like `Rack_ERCombo_Bench`. The bench hides for squat and deadlift. There are no separate squat, bench and deadlift areas, so the white squat stands (`Rack_ERSquatStands`) are not used for stations. This replaces the "separate side of the gym per lift" idea in the notes below
- Heavy steel with visible hole spacing and bolts
- Floor under the stations: plain **black rubber mats**, no wood platforms
- Barbells like the Ohio Power Bar (reference 6)
- Calibrated-style colored plates (red 25, blue 20, yellow 15, green 10)

Roblox has to run this on phones too, so the goal is the **feel** of these references, not photo-realism. Real details go where the player looks most: the rack, the bar, the plates and the floor under the lifter.

**Station visuals** (how the player and the bench look at a station for each lift) are a gameplay rule. They are recorded in `GAME_DESIGN.md` §34.12, decision 15.

### Overall gym interior

1. **Iron Built Gym tour with Ray Williams** (video): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oHdHsWUEgI
   A real powerlifting gym (Ashland, Virginia). Look at: the overall layout, rack rows, lighting, walls.
2. **American Barbell Club tour** (video): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrmvFI-QYD0
   A powerlifting club gym (Gainesville, Florida). Look at: platform arrangement, flooring, how cluttered it is.
3. **Super Training Gym, "Rust and Iron" episode** (Tim Ferriss): https://tim.blog/2017/02/05/rust-and-iron-episode-2-powerlifter-mark-bell/
   Mark Bell's "strongest gym in the west" in Sacramento. The video itself is on Tim Ferriss's YouTube channel. Look at: monster racks, specialty bars, raw garage feel.
4. **Inside Westside Barbell** (Vice feature, article): https://barbend.com/?p=26870
   The most famous hardcore powerlifting gym. Look at: the darkest, grittiest end of the style.

### Squat racks

5. **Rogue Monster Lite squat stands and racks**: https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-rigs-racks/squat-stands/ml-squat-stands
   Look at: 3x3" steel uprights, hole spacing, J-cups, safety arms. Click a product to see its photos.

### Barbells

6. **Rogue Ohio Power Bar**: https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-45lb-ohio-power-bar-stainless
   Look at: knurling pattern, center knurl, IPF knurl marks, sleeve shape.

### Weight plates

7. **Eleiko IPF Powerlifting Competition Plates**: https://eleiko.com/equipment/plates/powerlifting/3060350-25-eleiko-ipf-powerlifting-competition-plate-25-kg
   Look at: slim profile, IPF colors, rim shape. This is the standard meet plate look.

### Flooring and platforms

8. **Titan deadlift platforms**: https://www.titan.fitness/strength/strength-accessories/deadlift-platforms/
   Look at: wood center with rubber drop zones on the sides.

### Competition venue (for later: the local meet venue)

9. **SBD Sheffield Powerlifting Championships** (Eleiko story): https://eleiko.com/en/stories/sbd-sheffield-powerlifting-championships
   The biggest IPF meet stage. Look at: the stage platform, lighting, how the lifter stands out from a dark background.

### Roblox examples

10. **"Gym Build" by Warlords** (Roblox case study): https://warlords-portfolio.lovable.app/work/gym-build
    A realistic gym built in Roblox Studio with PBR materials and modular walls. It's a clean commercial gym, not gritty. Look at: how realistic Roblox can get, the modular wall approach and materials.
