## 4. Patterns across my cutscenes
1. ONE TEMPLATE, MANY SKINS. About 45 of the 66 use the same skeleton: hard cut (or white flash) into a giant star that snaps small (0.3 s zoom-out punch) -> 6-7 s slow phase (small star centred, one themed backdrop: nebula, clouds, water, fog, clock, staff lines) -> the aura's EMBLEM strobes 2-3 times in the last 1.5 s (lily, crown, moon, lotus, cross, DNA, compass, anchor, tree ...) -> the star stretches toward the lens -> full-screen WHITE flash -> the viewer shows the aura on the avatar. Length almost always 8.75-9.5 s. The SAME music/SFX track is re-used each time: cut-in BOOM from silence (SUB -10..-30), bed -18..-24 dB, sparkle (HIGH band) decays to about -70..-75 dB by +3 s, then a 4-5 s brightness riser to about -33 dB, final hit on the white frame. Only the colour, backdrop and emblem change.
2. Within this "global" band (1 in 10M to 1 in 444M) rarity barely changes length or VFX: nearly everything is ~9 s with VFX 2/5. What makes some memorable is not more particles but an IDEA: a character that talks (C7), a question or fake choice (C4, C12), a 3-beat story (C30: heart -> chains -> explodes), a scale reveal (C58 eyes), a world change (C5, C56), or a joke (C35, C60, C22, C59).
3. Special / challenged / event auras break the template and run 13-34 s (C3 29 s, C7 29 s, C12 27 s, C30 13 s, C41 13 s, C49 34 s). The long ones that work use SILENCE and pacing contrast (C3, C7, C12); the one that fails (C49) is just static cards.
4. Transitions: IN = hard cut on a bass hit (most) or a white flash fading into the scene; OUT = full-screen WHITE flash (most), a themed-colour flash (C40 red, C57 pink, C27 gold), a black frame with a giant lens flare (C52), or a fade to black/gray (C29, C65). The flash always lands on the loudest beat.
5. Sound/picture sync is exact: the cut-in frame and the BOOM are the same frame (0:30.4); the final white frame and the final hit are the same frame (0:39.8); brightness (HIGH band) climbs 4-5 s before the reveal. The best ones add a sudden DROP-OUT just before the big hit (C9 3:08-3:09.5 quiet white, C21 6:03.7 time stops, C3 1:17-1:18.5, C57 14:08.5, C12 digital silences).
6. No real 3D camera moves and NO screen shake in any cutscene here. Motion is faked: zoom-out punch at the start, the star rushing the lens at the end, foreground objects sliding past (C2 pillars, C8 smoke, C21 clock hand), slow drift. Depth comes from foreground parallax.
7. The player's avatar is NOT inside the cutscenes (except the live clips C5, C7 end, C33, C56). The avatar appears afterwards in the collection viewer (on a pedestal wearing the aura) or in the live world: the reveal is "white flash -> you, wearing it".
8. The info panel/unlock card always has the same layout: title in its own themed font and colour, "1 in N" under it, a tier word in colour (CHALLENGED white, EXALTED blue, GLORIOUS red, EVENT green, Unobtainable gray), conditions, credits; the newer card (C59, 14:39.5) adds "Close" and "View Cutscene" buttons. Glitch auras even scramble their own title letters (C29, C30).
9. The weak ones fail the same way: too dark to see (C14, C38, C55, C64), no emblem, nothing changes in the last seconds.
10. What other players see: live clips show the global in the shared world with bystanders (C33) and a server-wide white-out of the world (C56); weather lines appear in chat.
