## 4. Patterns across my cutscenes

- LENGTH grows with rarity, but not strictly: tiers up to ~1 in 450M are ~6-10 s template scenes; from ~1 in 670M
  many are bespoke 15-35 s pieces; the longest (COMPASS 46 s, Paradox: Collapse 37 s, E-Blast 34 s) are community
  contest pieces. The best-feeling ones are 10-30 s.
- THE GAME USES TEMPLATES. Four shared building blocks appear again and again with only the backdrop/colour swapped:
  (a) "star in a void" + white flash in/out (almost every official one), (b) the "build" track: flash -> 2-4 s of near
  silence -> a 6-7 s swell that peaks exactly on the white-out back to the game (Freedom, Despair, Fatal Error, Desync,
  Space, Non-Existence), (c) the "heavy" track: a loud bass hit on the flash, the top end dipping then climbing over
  6 s (Happppy, Flamereal, Cyber Love, Magical Doctor, Scarlet: Emperor, Brittleheart, Alleyway), (d) the
  "filter-sweep" track: steady bass with the HIGH band opening -60 -> -22 dB over 7 s, then rolling off again after the
  return so the game feels "muffled" (Luxuriant, Aquamarine, Forgotten, Delight, Anchor). Cheap way to make dozens.
- Standard skeleton of an official reveal: roll lands in the game (sting) -> FULL-SCREEN WHITE FLASH with a star
  (0.1-0.2 s) -> the themed "dimension" (one continuous shot, 6-12 s, the star always in the centre) -> the star swells
  -> WHITE-OUT -> back to the game with the UI HIDDEN for a 1-3 s hero shot of the avatar wearing the aura -> UI slides
  back. Sound peaks on the white-out.
- VFX: the amount goes up with rarity in the template tiers (1-2 -> 4), but the very top tiers often go DOWN to clean
  greyscale typography (Spica family, Chronoxus, Eden). Readability comes from: one centred focal point, one colour
  family, symmetry. Clutter happens with full-screen colour flicker (Desync, Fatal Error, OMEGA, Amethyst).
- CAMERA is mostly static or a slow push/drift; very few real cuts. The memorable ones move: Delight (fly-through
  tunnel), Anchor (dolly down rails), COMPASS (dive into the whirlpool), Chronoxus (rack focus, object flying past the
  lens), Scarlet: Emperor (low hero angle on return).
- THE WORLD CHANGES: several auras repaint the whole map before/after (Scarlet red world + giant title in the sky,
  Brittleheart ice forest, Magical Doctor giant pills, Spica crystal throne, OMEGA darkening the world).
- PLAYER AVATAR: almost never inside the dimension. The avatar appears only in the game shot before and in the hero
  shot after. The "item on the player" is the payoff.
- SOUND rules seen everywhere: the first hit lands exactly on the first flash/cut frame; silence (0.5-2 s, sometimes
  9 s) right before the biggest image; the loudest moment is the white-out; on return the music either keeps going or
  gets muffled. Clock ticks, heartbeats and beat-synced bars are used as build-up.
- TEXT: many top tiers tell a tiny story in 2-4 typed lines ("Now, let's bring back the lost light"; "Do you remember
  the fun times..."). The name is often the climax, written big (VIOLET, AMETHYST, E-BLAST, SPICA, the game's chrome
  "QUANTUM" title card).
- HUMOUR: the April Fools versions (Spicar, The Hot's Oden) copy a famous serious cutscene beat for beat and swap the
  payoff for something silly (a loading spinner + meme; a food cart + oden ingredients). Breakdown (fake crash) and
  Non-Existence ("NO SIGNAL") play with "is the game broken?". The joke only works because the serious one exists.
- UNLOCK SCREEN: Hot's RNG has no separate unlock card in these clips. The "card" is (a) the right-hand aura info
  panel that pops back with the name, and (b) for some auras a big stylised in-world title (STAR◆PRESSURE / QUANTUM /
  1 in 930,170,510; THE DISREGARDED DATA / UNKNOWN NON-EXISTENCE; SPECTRUM). The aura card also has a "Replay
  Cutscene" button (seen 19:21).
- Others: no other players or chat messages are visible in any clip in my range.

- Cluttered all-at-once VFX (OMEGA skull section 15:58-16:10) where nothing reads.
