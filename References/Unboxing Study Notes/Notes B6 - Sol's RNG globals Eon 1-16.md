### Recurring templates and tricks
1. White flash = the universal hand-back to the game (nearly every clip): 0.0-0.2 s full white, then a 0.6-1.0 s fade
   onto the avatar wearing the reward. The loudest point is usually on or just after this flash.
2. Opening: hard cut from the game into the cutscene with a bass BOOM in the first 0.3-0.8 s (measured on every clip).
3. Silence before the hit: Overture (2:36.8), Oppression (4:15.5), Dreammetric (0.7 s gaps before chapters, 3.5 s dead
   silence on NO SIGNAL), Lamenthyr stutter cuts (16:46.7-16:49.2), the phobia clip starting in near-silence (17:12.5).
4. Build with the HIGH band: shimmer climbing 25-35 dB over 4-6 s into the flash (template clips).
5. Colour drain = time stop: Overture greyscale 2:36.3; Mastermind turns the whole server greyscale 24:46.
6. Screen-crack overlay: Overture 2:37.3, Abyssal Hunter 9:25.3.
7. Words as hits, one per beat: WINTER/BLIZZARD/FROST (17:00-17:02.8), MUST/OBEY/ORDER (25:06-25:07), KING (19:40.8),
   NEXT STOP / THE NORTH POLE (20:40-20:41), "[ OVERDRIVE ]" (11:37).
8. Text that speaks to the player (Oppression, Unknown, Luminosity "WELL, YOU FINALLY REACHED ME.", the Dream Traveler
   conductor, Eden's grin "VERY WELL, WHAT DO YOU WANT?"). No spoken voice anywhere in the video: all text.
9. Giant beings shown in pieces: eyes in the dark (9:23.8, 18:46-18:49, 17:27.6), a grin (22:30), a serpent too big for
   the frame (19:42), giant fists (17:31).
10. Fake malfunction jokes: NO SIGNAL (7:42.5), "Wake Up." boxes (15:37), "Disconnected" box (24:12), glitch bars.
11. Families share a background (Zodiac/Legendarium 5:01-5:23, the green-splatter pair 11:19 / 14:37, the cube pair
    15:03 / 15:16, the Sovereign pair 14:12 / 16:52, Overture / Overture: History) so related items look related.
12. Real unlock screen: world dimmed; centred logo-styled name + one-line subtitle ("OBLIVION / The truth seeker");
    Equip (blue) / Skip (red); side toasts. Older: "You've found..." top-centre, name by the avatar.
13. What other players get: the cutscene itself (global), a chat line "<player> HAS FOUND <Aura>, CHANCE OF 1 IN <N>!",
    and for top auras the world changes for everyone (red fog 16:44, frozen greyscale 24:46, halo 18:31, comet 17:05).
    The chat fills with caps reactions (21:41).

### Cheap vs epic
- Cheap: 9 s of one small symbol on black (clips 6, 8, 23, 24, 42, 45, 49, 66); near-black frames unreadable even when
  brightened; flat untextured shapes (Glitch); one idea with nothing building.
- Epic: a build you can HEAR (ticks, heartbeat, drumroll), one clear break (silence / crack / colour drain), a reveal
  BIGGER than the frame, and an ending on the player's own avatar or on the real world changing.
- Clutter: Reality (12:31-12:35), Abomination (25:32-25:37), the Egg collage (23:10.5-23:11.5) are 5/5 and the subject
  gets lost, but each is short (1.5-5 s) and followed by a calm, clean image; that chaos -> calm rhythm worked.
