## 4. Patterns across my cutscenes
- FIVE TIERS of presentation, scaling with rarity (the video goes 10K -> 999M):
  1. Black + coloured vignette, NO star (Corrosive, Starlight, Lunar, Solar, Star Rider, Undefined): ~4 s, 1 flash.
  2. Star on black (Powered ... Comet): ~8 s; boom on the cut, small twisting star, grows, coloured vignette, flash.
  3. Star + 2 s WHITE-OUT + glass SHATTER (Exotic, Jade, Bounded, Celestial, Twilight, Kyawthuite, Astral, Galaxy):
     ~12 s; adds a held white blind and two crack bursts.
  4. Star over a coloured NIGHT SKY (Arcane ... Matrix): ~9 s; the sound drops to near-silence (with one lone
     crackle) before a 3-s riser.
  5. THEMED (Overture and above): ~9-10 s of themed particles + an emblem that pulses on the beat, the aura's own
     music; the very rarest add a WORLD PAYOFF (Nihility, Atlas, Ruins, Sovereign, Aegis, Memory, Oblivion): flash back
     into the real game world, which is TRANSFORMED (photo-negative, black world, white-out, a giant sword, a shield
     dome, a vortex), with the avatar there, and a server chat announcement.
- Length does NOT keep growing with rarity: tier 3 (12 s) is longer than tiers 4-5 (9-10 s). What grows is
  MEANING (themed particles, an emblem), MUSIC, and finally the WORLD changing. Only Oblivion is truly long (~45 s).
- The reveal card is the same for all 52: flash -> the index washed white -> NAME centred in a font that matches the
  aura (script for Celestial/Symphony/Ethereal, blocky logo for Comet, wide-spaced serif for ARCHANGEL/GARGANTUA, red
  serif with an emblem for STARSCOURGE, outlined caps for HYPER-VOLT) + one black "Close" pill under it; it holds
  1.5-2.75 s while the white fades to a dimmed screen (~1 s ease). No rarity word, no odds on the card itself (the
  odds live in the index side panel "1 in N"; Aegis's world payoff shows the odds as big text across the top).
- Sound always RISES INTO the flash; the loudest moment is always the reveal frame (mix -7..-13, sub -4..-15). Silence
  is used only as a short tension gap BEFORE the build (night-sky dip, Bloodlust's 0.4-s cut, Oblivion's eerie -51 bed),
  never right on the reveal. Booms land on: the cut-in, the white snap, each emblem pulse, the reveal. Cracks are
  HIGH-band only (glass sizzle), booms are SUB.
- Camera: the cutscene itself has NO camera (2D overlay, centred star); motion comes from the star shrinking/growing,
  particles drifting at different depths, and emblems popping. The index stage camera slowly orbits the avatar; the
  GARGANTUA preview is the one real camera move (drop to a low angle under a giant object, then pull far back and
  tilt). World payoffs use the normal 3rd-person play camera, with heavy motion blur/shake (Nihility) or a wide high
  shot (Sovereign 14:13).
- Colour: one aura colour per cutscene (star, sky, vignette, flash all share it); big moments invert or split colours
  (Matrix:Overdrive red -> teal, Sovereign blue | gold split, Exotic rainbow corners, Chromatic/Glitch cycling).
- What feels cheap: tiers 1-2 (an empty black screen for 3-5 s, the same star 30 times); the inserted "Credits"
  clips with different audio; cluttered world payoffs where you cannot find your avatar (Sovereign 14:03-14:12).
- What feels epic: a themed emblem pulsing ON a bass hit; particles that keep going over the name card (Archangel);
  the world itself changing; scale (a black hole over a tiny avatar, a pillar-sized sword next to you); the game
  speaking ("HAVE YOU SEEN MY LAST MEMORY?", words "WAKE", "FROM" floating in the world); a count-up montage that
  accelerates; a thing rushing at the camera then white-out.
- Funny / over-the-top: Nihility = 9 s of literally nothing, then a red explosion; Sailor = a full serious build-up
  revealing a tiny boat; the game asking the player a dramatic question. These work because the build-up is played
  completely straight.
- What other players see: a yellow server-wide chat line naming the player and the aura (Nihility 8:37.1; Atlas,
  Ruins, Sovereign, Memory, Oblivion world clips also show chat lines top-left); the transformed world is visible in
  the shared server (the clips are filmed by players standing in the world).
