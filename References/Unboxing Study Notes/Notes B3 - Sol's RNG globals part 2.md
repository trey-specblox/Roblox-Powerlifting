## 4. Patterns across my cutscenes
1. Length grows with rarity: 1 in 450M-935M = 9-12 s (one locked shot); 1 in 1B-1.2B = 15-26 s (several shots, text); 1 in 1.45B-7.5B = 20-41 s (multi-shot films, letterbox, title cards). Event/holiday auras are 10-21 s regardless of rarity.
2. One hero light + one idea. Below 1 in 1B nearly all are the same star template with a colour, a backdrop and ONE extra (a prop, a word, a punchline). The memorable ones are the ones with the extra.
3. VFX amount does NOT climb with rarity. The rarest are often calmer (Luminosity 3, Astraios 3, Master Hand 4); the VFX-5 ones (Equinox, Matrix : Reality, Pixelation) are the most tiring. Big moments use a full-white frame (0.3 s up to 4 s) more than particles.
4. Black in, white out: every cutscene enters from black (0.3-2 s) and almost every one exits through a WHITE FLASH into the reveal.
5. Sound template: boom on the first image, decay, second hit, 4-5 s brightness riser, peak exactly on the flash. Rarer ones switch to music tracks (credited on screen for the top one) and use silence as a tool (Breakthrough 3 s silence before the first shot; Nyctophobia's no-highs drone then sting; Matrix : Reality's quiet sky after the storm).
6. Scale and characters appear only at the top: a sea-dragon "KING" too big for the frame, an armoured knight shown in pieces, a mech giant in fire, a ring of watching eyes. Characters never speak aloud; they "speak" in typewriter or big outlined text (Luminosity, the Christmas train).
7. The player's own avatar is almost never inside the cutscene (3 of 53: P.U.K.E.K.O.G.O.D. ending in-world, Astraios after the dragon, the in-world one). The aura-on-avatar reveal is left to the menu/world after the flash.
8. Camera: the template is locked off; rarer ones use push-ins (Matrix : Reality, Ascendant), fly-throughs (Yolkegg tunnel, Luminosity pillars), tracking (Sky Festival ship), orbit (in-world one), fast montage cuts (Eggis, Breakthrough, Equinox), letterbox bars (Winter Garden, Astraios, Breakthrough). No screen shake seen anywhere in my range.
9. Words as beats: theme words one per second (PEACE/ORDER, WINTER/BLIZZARD/FROST), titles built word by word ("THE / ABSOLUTE / RADIANCE"), a title card with the item name ("KING", "Master Hand", "MASTERHAND / Dimensional Crafted").
10. Humour works best short and deadpan: "Wake Up." after a dreamy star, the PowerPoint "it got moderated, sadly" slides, "youareanidiot" smileys. The 27 s silent "syncing" screen just feels broken.
