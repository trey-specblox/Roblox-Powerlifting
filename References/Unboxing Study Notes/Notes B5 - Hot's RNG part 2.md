## 4. Patterns across my cutscenes
1. Length does NOT grow with rarity inside the official game: the Fortune's End template is 12-13 s for 1 in 10 AND
   1 in 2000; the negative template is 11-12 s for -22 million and -500 million. Rarity changes COLOUR and ORNAMENT
   (Perlica = gold + magic circles + light shafts + a light pillar on the avatar), not length. The 30-46 s ones are
   special community/fan pieces with a story.
2. Two official templates: (a) world -> white flash -> coloured star in a vortex with a "??? ???" name-scramble row
   -> star grows -> white -> world with aura; (b) world -> a giant star fills the frame and shrinks to centre while a
   themed backdrop appears -> star grows back over the frame -> white -> world. Both are bookended by WHITE flashes
   and return to the same static world camera. Every scene centres a 4- or 8-point STAR (the aura symbol).
3. Sound starts HARD: almost every cutscene starts with a full-loudness HIT/BOOM on its first frame (mix -16 to -23
   at once). Only Star Pressure, Endearment and Vanquished fade/crescendo in, and those feel the most cinematic.
4. Most music is a flat wall (mix -18 to -22 for the whole cutscene, +/-2 dB). The memorable moments TAKE SOMETHING
   AWAY first: Endearment (no bass for 17 s, then +48 dB bass on the white flash), Abandoned (8 dB duck for 1 s
   while the star grows, bass back on the flare), Cybercrush (0.9 s black silence, then boom on the heart), Despair:
   Demon (bass-only drone for 3 s, treble arrives on the white flash), CyberFantasia and Space V2 (treble muffled,
   then opened on the white-out). Ephemeral reverses it: the climax IS total silence.
5. The big hit lands on a FULL-WHITE (sometimes red or lilac) frame, and the reveal (title or aura) comes 0.2-0.5 s
   later out of the white. White-outs fade over 0.5-1 s.
6. Scale punch: emblems and hearts arrive OVERSIZED and shrink to rest in ~0.5 s (Nightbringer 21:12.8, Cybercrush
   22:27, negative star 24:02.8); titles arrive tilted/glitched and settle (Endearment 37:06.8 -> 37:07.5, Space
   30:32.0 -> 30:32.3).
7. One hue per aura; negative (cursed) rarities go black-and-white/desaturated with camcorder dates, VHS, errors.
8. The player's avatar is in only ~8 of 45, usually as a flat silhouette; the two best (Space V2, Despair: Demon)
   put the avatar at the centre of the climax.
9. Cheap = flat music start to end, one effect looping (Violintine, Freedom: Pixel, Suspended), focus-less glitch
   clutter, abrupt cut (Despair: Demon Jackz 34:20.7). Epic = a held-back element returning on the reveal, one clear
   hero image (rose, heart, avatar, villain), a title on the beat, a short white-out, then calm.
10. Funny/over-the-top played straight: Fatal Error (a tornado of Windows error boxes), Breakdown (fake blue screen),
   Musicalty ("ALL FOUR MERGED INTO ONE WITH THE POWER OF SOUND!", "COMBO x4!!"), Neuromecha ("Ready for battle!",
   "LAUNCHING^^"), "YOU CAN'T HACK NOW", the "scrapped" meme intro. They work because the music is full volume and
   nothing winks at the camera: the user's "so serious it's funny" target.
